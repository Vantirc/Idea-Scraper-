# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Data

*Honest note upfront: This dataset is thin. Most posts lack excerpts, so signal comes from titles + engagement only. Only 3–4 clusters have genuine evidence. I'm not padding.*

---

## 🥇 1. AI Coding Agent Infrastructure & Orchestration

**Pain point:** Developers using AI coding agents lack reliable tools for managing context, switching between agents, and maintaining a "source of truth" across sessions.

**Evidence strength:** 4 posts, ~24 engagement, highly specific asks

- "Continue coding agent is dead. Alternatives?" — direct tool-switching pain
- "Show HN: Active Source of Truth for Your Coding Agents" (meetless.ai)
- "Show HN: A complete companion for agentic development – Burmese"
- "Ask HN: How do you host your vibe coded tools/sites?"
- "Show HN: One portal for all your MCP servers"

**Who'd pay:** Individual developers and small engineering teams paying $10–50/mo; also enterprise dev tooling budgets.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49398366
- https://meetless.ai
- https://www.theburmese.xyz/
- https://onemcp.dev/
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49393465

## 🥈 2. Video/Audio Transcript Access & Summarization

**Pain point:** People want to read or search through YouTube and TED talk content without sitting through full videos, but there's no clean unified tool for it.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, ~14 engagement, very specific ask

- "Ask HN: Anyone set up ways to easily obtain and read transcripts from Ted, YT?" — textbook "wish there was a tool for X"
- "Ask HN: What websites do you visit daily to stay informed?" — related curation need

**Who'd pay:** Knowledge workers, researchers, students, newsletter writers. Freemium SaaS at $8–20/mo is proven (Snipd, Readwise already exist — but gap remains in unified cross-platform transcript search).

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49405684
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49406588

## 🥉 3. Developer-Facing GPU Capacity Discovery

**Pain point:** Developers and small AI teams can't easily find available GPU compute capacity across providers without manually checking each one.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, ~12 engagement, specific infrastructure pain

- "Where are people finding GPU capacity?" — explicit market search
- "Ask HN: Is it time to run the LLM engines on the CPU?" — workaround signal (people exploring CPU inference *because* GPU access is painful)

**Who'd pay:** ML engineers, AI startups, indie hackers running inference. A GPU spot-market aggregator or broker could charge commission or subscription.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49403759
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49402551

## ⚠️ Honorable Mention: Border Device Search Privacy Tools

**Pain point:** Travelers (especially tech workers) are anxious about US border agents searching phones and want a defensible solution.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 25 engagement — *high engagement but single data point*

This is a real pain and the engagement is the highest of any "Ask HN" here, but one post isn't a cluster. Worth watching. Existing solutions (duress modes, travel profiles) are clunky.

**Source:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49397441
