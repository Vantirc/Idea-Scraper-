# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Signal

---

## 1. 🔥 FIRE / Financial Independence Planning Tools
**Pain point:** People want a simple, personalized calculator to know exactly when they can stop working.

**Evidence:** 1 post, **112 engagement** (highest by far), direct Show HN with working tool

**Specificity:** High — concrete tool built, strong community validation via engagement

**Who'd pay:** Salaried professionals 28–45, FIRE community, personal finance SaaS users

**Links:**
- https://github.com/karmanyaahm/budget-tools/tree/main/fire

---

## 2. 💼 Better Job Boards for Tech Workers
**Pain point:** LinkedIn is low-quality and existing job boards are failing technical users.

**Evidence:** 2 posts, **54 combined engagement**, direct "Ask HN: What job boards are good these days?"

**Specificity:** High — explicit dissatisfaction + direct request for alternatives

**Who'd pay:** Recruiters, tech companies hiring, job seekers (freemium model)

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49816807
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49816567

---

## 3. 🤖 AI Skill Atrophy vs. Productivity Tradeoff
**Pain point:** Developers are genuinely conflicted about whether AI coding tools are making them worse engineers long-term.

**Evidence:** 3 posts, **51 combined engagement** — AI offloading debate, "not reading code" concern, AI client frustration

**Specificity:** Medium — anxiety is real but product shape is fuzzy (could be: deliberate practice tools, AI usage dashboards, hybrid coding environments)

**Who'd pay:** Engineering managers, developers who want to stay sharp, bootcamps/training platforms

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49820486
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49819873
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49826029

---

## 4. 🔒 Secret/Token Leakage Prevention in AI Coding Tools
**Pain point:** Developers using Claude Code, Copilot, and similar tools are worried about accidentally sending secrets/API keys to AI providers.

**Evidence:** 2 posts, **10 combined engagement** — Tokenhush (built tool), compaction.dev (context management)

**Specificity:** High — working tools already exist, indicating real demand; security is a compliance concern

**Who'd pay:** Enterprise dev teams, security-conscious startups, DevSecOps tools buyers

**Links:**
- https://github.com/fregie/tokenhush
- https://github.com/philipppohlmann/compaction

---

## 5. 💰 AI Coding Tool Cost Comparison
**Pain point:** Developers and teams don't have a clear way to compare the real cost of GitHub Copilot vs. Claude vs. other AI coding assistants.

**Evidence:** 1 post, **7 engagement**, direct explicit question

**Specificity:** High — very concrete ask, no good answer exists publicly

**Who'd pay:** CTOs, engineering managers, procurement teams at SMBs and enterprises

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49813172

---

## 6. 🧪 Local Lambda/ECS Testing Before Deploy
**Pain point:** Developers lack good tooling to test serverless/container code locally before pushing to AWS.

**Evidence:** 1 post, **3 engagement** — low signal but a known chronic pain point in DevOps

**Specificity:** Medium — question is specific, but engagement is weak here

**Who'd pay:** Backend/DevOps engineers, AWS-heavy startups, platform engineering teams

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49820593

---

## ⚠️ Honest Assessment

**Clusters 1–3** have genuine signal worth acting on.

**Clusters 4–5** are specific and buildable but thin on volume — worth monitoring, not betting on yet.

**Cluster 6** is a known pain point but this single low-engagement post adds little new evidence. Existing tools (LocalStack, SAM CLI) already address it — differentiation would be needed.

**Dropped:** Wine Atlas, social media site, tmux optimization, wallpaper app, C3 playground, Jev hype posts — noise, one-off projects, or no clear monetizable pain.
