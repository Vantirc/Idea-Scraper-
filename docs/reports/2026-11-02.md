# Business Idea Clusters from HN/Reddit Data

---

## 1. 🔒 Privacy-First Smart Home / IoT Blocking

**Pain point:** People want to use modern TVs and IoT devices without being surveilled, and have no good tools to block tracking at the network level.

**Evidence strength:** 🟢 Strong
- 2 posts, combined engagement **~1,344**
- "Ask HN: Which TV do you recommend for privacy?" (1,341 engagement — massive signal)
- "Ask HN: uBlock Origin for the IoT devices in my house?" (3 engagement)
- Highly specific asks, not vague frustration

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-conscious consumers, home lab hobbyists, parents, small businesses — anyone who buys a Pi-hole but wants something easier. Also: enterprise IoT security teams.

**Possible products:**
- Consumer router firmware / appliance with IoT-specific blocklists
- Managed "privacy router" subscription (like Eero but privacy-first)
- IoT traffic auditing dashboard

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50328896
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50333058

## 2. 🤖 AI Vendor Lock-in / Price Rug-Pull Insurance

**Pain point:** Developers and startups are building on top of AI APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) and are terrified of sudden price hikes or deprecations destroying their unit economics.

**Evidence strength:** 🟡 Moderate
- 2 posts, combined engagement **~37**
- "What is your backup plan when AI companies rug pull their prices?" (15)
- "Do you have a Plan B?" (22) — context strongly implies AI dependency risk
- Specific, recurring anxiety in the builder community

**Who'd pay:** SaaS founders, indie hackers, dev agencies building AI-powered products — anyone whose COGS is dominated by LLM API costs.

**Possible products:**
- LLM routing/abstraction layer (switch providers automatically on price/quality)
- Cost monitoring + alerting for AI API spend
- Local model fallback orchestration service

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50336034
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50334841

## 3. 🖥️ Local LLM Hardware & Infrastructure Tooling

**Pain point:** Developers want to run LLMs locally for privacy, cost, or reliability reasons but need guidance on hardware and tooling to make it practical.

**Evidence strength:** 🟡 Moderate
- 2 posts, combined engagement **~18**
- "What hardware are you using to run local LLMs?" (10)
- "I indexed 37h of YouTube using RTX 4090 and local ML models" (8)
- Practical, hands-on signal — people are already doing this and sharing results

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-focused developers, enterprises with data residency requirements, AI hobbyists, researchers.

**Possible products:**
- Curated local LLM hardware buying guide + benchmarking service (subscription or affiliate)
- Pre-configured local AI appliance (think: "local AI in a box")
- Managed self-hosted LLM setup service for small businesses

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50337431
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50329475

## 4. 💼 AI Coding Agent Oversight & Audit Tools

**Pain point:** Developers are using AI coding agents but don't trust them — they want visibility into what the agent plans to do before it does damage.

**Evidence strength:** 🟡 Moderate
- 2 posts, combined engagement **~11**
- "Do you read your coding agent's plans before approving?" (7)
- "What mistakes did AI make in your codebase?" (4)
- Niche but highly specific; the "mistakes" post shows real, felt pain

**Who'd pay:** Engineering teams at companies using Cursor, Claude Code, Copilot Workspace, Devin — anyone with a real codebase to protect.

**Possible products:**
- AI agent change audit log + diff review UI
- Policy-as-code layer that blocks agents from touching sensitive files/modules
- "Agent PR review" bot that explains what an agent changed and why

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50334078
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50328797

## 5. 📈 Lightweight / Free TradingView Alternative

**Pain point:** Traders and developers want professional charting tools without TradingView's pricing or feature bloat.

**Evidence strength:** 🔴 Weak-but-real
- 1 post, engagement **8**
- "Show HN: Aulico, my free TradingView alternative" — someone already built this
- The fact it was built and shown suggests validated demand, but engagement is low here

**Who'd pay:** Retail traders, quant hobbyists, fintech startups needing embeddable charts.

**Note:** Market exists (TradingView is ~$60–$600/yr) but competition is stiff. The signal here is the builder, not the crowd.

**Sources:**
- https://www.aulico.com

## ⚠️ Honest Assessment

**Clusters 1 and 2 have the strongest signal.** Cluster 1 especially — 1,341 engagement on a single HN post is exceptional and indicates a large, underserved audience actively seeking solutions. Clusters 3 and 4 are real but niche. Cluster 5 is thin evidence.

The remaining posts (job boards, vibe-coding curiosity, geopolitics, creative side projects) were dropped — no clear business signal.

**Bottom line: pursue the privacy/IoT and AI vendor lock-in angles. The demand is loud and the existing solutions are painful.**
