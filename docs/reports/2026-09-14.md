# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Data

---

## Honest Assessment First

This dataset is **thin**. Most posts have very low engagement, no excerpts, and many are off-topic or noise. Only **3 clusters** have meaningful signal. I'm not padding this out.

---

## Cluster 1: AI Agent Security & Credential Management

**Idea Name:** MCP Auth / Agent Credential Isolation

**Pain Point:** Developers running AI agents via MCP (Model Context Protocol) need to authorize tool calls and manage credentials without exposing secrets directly to agents.

**Evidence:**
- 2 posts on the same GitHub repo (mcp-auth-keydris-template) — duplicate submission suggests real interest
- "Show HN: Authorize MCP tool calls without giving agents the credentials" (×2, eng: 3 each)
- Adjacent signal: "Ask HN: What are privacy/security tools you wish existed?" (eng: 12)
- Adjacent: "Ask HN: Multi-agent workflows in production" (eng: 6)
- **Total engagement: ~24 | Specificity: High**

**Who'd Pay:** Platform/infra teams shipping agentic products; enterprises deploying LLM workflows; DevOps/security engineers.

**Sources:**
- https://github.com/keydrisLabs/mcp-auth-keydris-template
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49690524
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49689454

## Cluster 2: Privacy & Security Tooling Gaps

**Idea Name:** Unbuilt Privacy/Security Tools

**Pain Point:** Developers and privacy-conscious users can clearly articulate security tools they want but that don't exist yet — a recurring ask on HN that draws community responses.

**Evidence:**
- "Ask HN: What are privacy/security tools you wish exist, but don't yet?" (eng: 12)
- "Ask HN: How is AI actively a threat to humanity if it's only online?" (eng: 16) — shows anxiety about AI-era threat surface
- "Ask HN: Do you worry about fires in AI datacenters?" (eng: 13) — infrastructure risk awareness
- **Total engagement: ~41 | Specificity: Medium** (the wish-list thread is direct but no excerpts to see specifics)

**Who'd Pay:** Privacy-focused consumers, security professionals, B2B compliance teams.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49690524
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49689905
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49692215

---

## Cluster 3: Anti-AI Creative Platform

**Idea Name:** Human-Only Creative Community

**Pain Point:** A segment of young creatives actively wants to exclude AI from their work and tooling, but no dedicated platform or marketplace exists to serve or certify them.

**Evidence:**
- "Ask HN: Would a startup for young creatives who reject AI be feasible?" (eng: 26) — the question *is* the business idea, posed directly
- Engagement is moderate and the ask is highly specific
- **Total engagement: 26 | Specificity: Very High**

**Who'd Pay:** Human artists, illustrators, writers selling "AI-free" work; brands wanting authentic human creative work; platforms needing a trust signal.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49689696
