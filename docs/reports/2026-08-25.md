# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Data

> **Honest upfront note:** This dataset is thin. Most posts have very low engagement and no excerpts to analyze. Only 2–3 clusters have meaningful signal. I'm not padding to 8.

---

## 🥇 Cluster 1: Side Project Monetization Tooling

**Idea:** Platform/community for founders earning $100–$1K/month from side projects

**Pain point:** Solo builders lack structured support (planning, accountability, pricing guidance) to go from $0 → consistent side income.

**Evidence:**
- "Those making $500/month on side projects" — 136 engagement
- "How Much to Charge – Freelance/Contracting" — 3 engagement
- "Hierarchical to-do calendar for solo founders" — 4 engagement
- **3 posts, ~143 total engagement, moderately specific**

**Who'd pay:** Indie hackers, freelancers, early solopreneurs. Would pay for templates, community, or a focused planning tool ($10–$30/mo).

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49417766
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49427649
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49428450

## 🥈 Cluster 2: AI Reliability & Fallback Infrastructure

**Idea:** Status monitoring + fallback routing layer for AI API dependencies

**Pain point:** Teams building on AI APIs (Claude, etc.) have no graceful fallback when providers go down, causing production outages.

**Evidence:**
- "Claude Is Down?" — 48 engagement
- "When Claude is down, do they have a backup Claude?" — 9 engagement
- "What AI companies provide human support?" — 4 engagement
- **3 posts, ~61 total engagement, very specific operational ask**

**Who'd pay:** Dev teams and startups with AI-dependent products. Classic infrastructure pain — would pay for uptime SLAs, automatic failover routing, and alerting ($50–$500/mo).

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49415325
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49415517
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49423377

## 🥉 Cluster 3: Passive Screen/Context Memory Tool

**Idea:** Lightweight ambient logging that captures what you're working on as searchable text — no screenshots

**Pain point:** People lose context on past work sessions and can't easily recall what they were doing or decided without heavy tooling.

**Evidence:**
- "Screen memory without screenshots, just text to Markdown" — 58 engagement
- "How I Read Books" (knowledge retention angle) — 18 engagement
- **2 posts, ~76 engagement, one concrete product already being built**

**Who'd pay:** Developers, researchers, knowledge workers who do deep async work. Privacy-conscious users who reject screenshot tools like Rewind. ~$8–$15/mo.

**Links:**
- https://github.com/dragthelake/ambient-context
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49426275
