# Business Idea Clusters from HN/Reddit Data

---

## Honest Assessment First

This dataset is **thin**. Most posts have low engagement, no excerpts, and many are meta-complaints about HN/Reddit UI. Only **3 clusters** have meaningful signal. I'm not padding this out.

---

## Cluster 1: AI Agent Infrastructure & Tooling

**Pain point:** AI agents need proper infrastructure (email, session sharing, decision management) that doesn't exist yet as polished products.

**Evidence strength:** 4 posts, ~15 combined engagement, moderately specific

| Post | Engagement |
|---|---|
| Pidgeon – Email inboxes for AI agents | 7 |
| Kolo – share agent sessions like Google Docs | 2 |
| Nakama – AI workspace for team-shared agents | 2 |
| Nouli – multi-turn decision graph harness | 4 |

**Who pays:** Dev teams and companies deploying AI agents in production; the "share agents across a company" framing targets SMB/enterprise

**Links:**
- https://pidgeon.ai
- https://github.com/komovaar/kolo
- https://github.com/ahmadrosid/nakama
- https://github.com/standing-desks/nouli

---

## Cluster 2: Travel Planning (Spreadsheet Replacement)

**Pain point:** Travelers still rely on messy spreadsheets because no existing tool fits the way people actually plan trips.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 8 engagement, but the *title itself* is the pain point signal ("got tired of travel spreadsheets") — a known, recurring complaint with validated competition (Wanderlog, Notion templates, etc.)

| Post | Engagement |
|---|---|
| wherewhen.cc – travel planner app | 8 |

**Who pays:** Frequent travelers, travel bloggers, group trip organizers — consumer SaaS or freemium

**Caveat:** Crowded space. The signal here is weak *from this dataset* but validated externally.

**Links:**
- https://www.wherewhen.cc

---

## Cluster 3: Third-Party API Reliability / Breaking Changes

**Pain point:** Developers are spending significant time reactively fixing broken integrations every time a third-party API ships breaking changes.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 2 engagement — **low**, but the framing ("turning into a full-time job") is a precise, operational pain point, not vague frustration

| Post | Engagement |
|---|---|
| Breaking changes in third-party APIs are turning into a full-time job | 2 |

**Who pays:** Engineering teams at companies with many integrations (fintech, SaaS platforms, agencies); existing players like Stedi or Konfig show this is monetizable

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50273438
