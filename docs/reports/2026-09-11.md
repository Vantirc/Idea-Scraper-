## 🥇 1. AI Privacy Control Manager

**Pain:** AI platforms (OpenAI et al.) silently re-enable data-training opt-outs, and users have no reliable way to audit or enforce their privacy settings across services.

**Evidence Strength:** 🔴 Strong
- 1 post, but **638 engagement** — the highest by 8x in this dataset
- Very specific, actionable complaint
- Recurring pattern (OpenAI has done this multiple times)

**Who'd Pay:** Privacy-conscious professionals, legal/compliance teams, enterprises using AI APIs, GDPR-regulated businesses

**The Product:** A monitoring service that regularly audits your AI platform settings and alerts you (or auto-resets) when privacy preferences are changed without consent.

**Sources:**
- [Tell HN: OpenAI keeps re-enabling the 'allow training' setting](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49643556)

## 🥈 2. AI Writing Assistant for Word / Docs (Non-Code)

**Pain:** Tools like Cursor transformed coding workflows, but no equivalent exists for prose/document work in Word, Google Docs, or legal/business writing contexts.

**Evidence Strength:** 🟡 Moderate
- 1 direct post (11 engagement) + supported by "Desktop AI assistant" post (4 engagement)
- Question is very specific and clearly formulated ("Why doesn't a Cursor for Word exist?")
- A known gap actively discussed in productivity circles

**Who'd Pay:** Lawyers, consultants, business analysts, writers, anyone living in Word/Docs

**The Product:** An AI pair-writer for long-form documents — context-aware edits, inline suggestions, track-changes integration, style enforcement.

**Sources:**
- [Why doesn't a Cursor for Word exist?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49646170)
- [Is there a genuine space for the Desktop AI assistant?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49645248)

## 🥉 3. AI Agent Reliability / Orchestration Tooling

**Pain:** Running AI agents in production is brittle — agents fail, go off-task, or can't complete real workflows (as evidenced by "3 of 11 agents finished").

**Evidence Strength:** 🟡 Moderate
- 3 related posts, ~12 combined engagement
- Includes a real test result (11 agents, only 3 finished — a concrete failure signal)
- Supported by agent workflow question and FinOps for LLM post

**Who'd Pay:** Startups building on AI agents, dev teams running automation pipelines, anyone paying per-token at scale

**The Product:** Agent reliability layer — monitoring, retry logic, cost controls, task completion auditing, and orchestration tooling for multi-agent workflows.

**Sources:**
- [I pointed 11 cold AI agents at my own product. 3 finished](https://pact0.com/notes/agents-vs-our-own-product)

**Who'd Pay:** Community managers, dev tool companies (GitHub, Jira), forum platforms, enterprise Slack admins

**The Product:** A classifier/plugin that flags or collapses AI-generated filler in comments, PRs, tickets, and forum threads.

**Sources:**
