# Validated Business Ideas from HN Data

---

## ⚠️ Honest Assessment First

Most posts here are **discussion/venting** with low engagement and no excerpts to confirm specificity. Only **3 clusters** have meaningful signal. The rest would be padding.

---

## 🥇 Cluster 1: Job Search Is a Broken, Manual Nightmare

**Pain point:** Tech job hunting feels like repetitive data-entry — filling the same fields across dozens of ATS portals with no intelligence or automation.

**Evidence strength:**
- 3 posts, combined engagement: **31**
- Titles are remarkably specific and redundant (two nearly identical posts appeared independently)
- High engagement relative to this dataset

**Posts:**
- "Looking for a tech job is a helpless data-entry nightmare" (12)
- "Tech market feels like a data entry job" (7)
- "Ask HN: What are your career plans in the age of AI?" (21 — career anxiety feeds same need)

**Who'd pay:** Mid-level tech workers actively job hunting; recruiters running outreach. $10–30/mo SaaS or per-application credit model.

**Opportunity angle:** AI-powered job application assistant that auto-fills ATS forms, tailors resumes per listing, and tracks applications — with a focus on *reducing the data-entry feel* (the specific complaint). Not another job board; a workflow layer on top of existing ones.

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50254873
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50254751

## 🥈 Cluster 2: AI Model Subscription Trust & Support Crisis

**Pain point:** Paying users of AI tools (specifically Claude/Anthropic) feel they have no recourse when billing, limits, or support fail them.

**Evidence strength:**
- 2 posts, combined engagement: **11**
- Both posts are *specific complaints* about the same vendor
- Signals a gap in AI-tier transparency and customer advocacy tooling

**Posts:**
- "Ask HN: Why Is Anthropic's Customer Support Bad?" (6)
- "Ask HN: What shenanigans is Anthropic pulling with Claude subs this time?" (5)

**Who'd pay:** Power users of AI tools ($20–200/mo spenders) who need usage transparency, billing alerts, and limit forecasting across multiple AI subscriptions.

**Opportunity angle:** An "AI subscription dashboard" — tracks spend, usage limits, rate limit patterns, and downtime across OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini etc. Think *Downdetector + billing transparency* for AI APIs. Could expand to enterprise AI cost management.

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50252272
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50252654

## 🥉 Cluster 3: Reviewing AI-Generated Code Faster Than Humans Can Inspect It

**Pain point:** Agentic AI coding tools produce output so fast that human review becomes the bottleneck — developers can't keep up with what the agent actually did.

**Evidence strength:**
- 2 posts, combined engagement: **8**
- One post is *unusually specific* about the workflow problem
- Corroborated by "What are you using for agentic coding now?" discussion

**Posts:**
- "Reviewing agent output arriving faster than you can inspect it" (4)
- "What are you using for agentic coding now?" (4)

**Who'd pay:** Senior engineers and tech leads at companies adopting agentic coding tools (Cursor, Claude Code, Devin etc.). Team/seat-based pricing, $30–100/seat/mo.

**Opportunity angle:** A **code review layer purpose-built for AI agent output** — diffs summarized in plain English, risk scoring per change, rollback suggestions, audit trails. Not a general code review tool; specifically designed for the *velocity and opacity* of agent-generated code.

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50248804
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50254273

*Bottom line: This dataset is thin. The job search cluster is the only one with strong, repeated, specific signal. Clusters 2 and 3 are worth watching but need more data before betting on them.*
