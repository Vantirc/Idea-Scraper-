# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Scrape

> **Honest caveat up front:** This dataset is thin. Most posts have no excerpts, making signal inference limited to titles alone. Only 2–3 clusters have real evidence worth acting on. I've noted confidence accordingly rather than padding to 8.

---

## 🥇 Cluster 1: AI Fatigue & Cognitive Overhead

**Idea name:** AI Workflow Clarity Tool

**Pain point:** Developers are overwhelmed by the pace and complexity of AI tooling, struggling to evaluate what's actually worth using.

**Evidence strength:**
- 3 posts pointing at this
- **223 + 15 + 4 = 242 total engagement** (dominant signal in the dataset)
- "Are you tired too in this AI era?" (223 pts) is the standout post

**Who'd pay:** Indie devs, tech leads, and engineering managers who need a curated/opinionated layer on top of AI tooling chaos — think "Wirecutter for AI dev tools" or a fatigue-aware onboarding product.

**Sources:**
- [Ask HN: Are you tired too in this AI era?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50301479) — 223 eng
- [Ask HN: How are you using AI in your personal life?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50292611) — 4 eng
- [Ask HN: True or False AI Isn't Replacing SWEs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50292620) — 3 eng

## 🥈 Cluster 2: AI Agent Workflow Orchestration

**Idea name:** Agent Workflow Composer

**Pain point:** Developers building with AI agents have no standard mental model or tooling for orchestrating multi-agent pipelines (swarms, sequential, orchestrated).

**Evidence strength:**
- 3 posts, **~27 total engagement**
- Specific and technical asks — these are people actively building, not just theorizing
- "Agent swarm, orchestra, sequential, what's your workflow?" is a clear gap signal

**Who'd pay:** AI-native startups, enterprise automation teams, solo devs shipping agentic products.

**Sources:**
- [Ask HN: Agent swarm, orchestra, sequential, what's your workflow?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50292324) — 6 eng
- [Show HN: Apache 2.0 Registry to let agents share skills](https://github.com/AgenticResourceRegistry/AgenticResourceRegistry) — 10 eng
- [Ask HN: Are you using a custom harness?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50301901) — 6 eng

## 🥉 Cluster 3: AI Tool Privacy & Data Leakage

**Idea name:** AI Tool Data Firewall / Audit Layer

**Pain point:** AI coding tools (e.g. Claude Code) silently leak sensitive user data like email addresses to third-party websites without disclosure.

**Evidence strength:**
- 1 post, **34 engagement**
- Highly specific and alarming disclosure — not vague frustration
- Single post but the signal is crisp; privacy violations by AI tools are a repeatable, growing problem

**Who'd pay:** Enterprise security teams, compliance-focused engineering orgs, SOC2-bound startups who need an audit/proxy layer between AI tooling and production environments.

**Sources:**
- [Tell HN: Claude Code adds your email to the prompt](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50286306) — 34 eng

## ⚠️ Cluster 4: AI-Assisted Video Editing

**Idea name:** AI Video Editor (Cursor-style)

**Pain point:** Video editing has no equivalent of AI-assisted coding — tab-complete and agent-driven edits don't exist in mainstream tools.

**Evidence strength:**
- 1 post (a Show HN), **60 engagement**
- Someone already built this (Arsaze) — validates demand exists, but also means the space is occupied
- Engagement is moderate; unclear if it's curiosity or real pull

**Who'd pay:** Content creators, YouTubers, indie filmmakers, marketing teams.

**Sources:**
- [Show HN: Arsaze – Cursor for video editing](https://www.arsaze.com) — 60 eng

## Bottom Line

**Only Clusters 1–3 have meaningful evidence.** Cluster 1 is the strongest raw signal by far but is diffuse — the business angle needs sharpening. Cluster 3 is the most *specific* pain point and the most actionable for a B2B security/compliance product. Cluster 2 is the most technically grounded with active builders looking for answers.

**Don't build from this dataset alone.** Validate with 10 direct conversations before writing code.
