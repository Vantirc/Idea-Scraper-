## 1. 🤖 AI Agent Guardrails & Control

**Pain point:** Teams deploying AI agents can't reliably constrain their behavior or grant least-privilege access to resources without breaking functionality.

**Evidence**
- "Show HN: OpenAPPA – deterministic guardrails that don't break agents" (32 engagement)
- "Ask HN: Allow agents access to cloud files with least privilege?" (5 engagement)
- "Ask HN: Do you think AI agents can escape human control?" (9 engagement)
- **3 posts | ~46 total engagement | Specific technical ask**

**Who pays:** Enterprise teams shipping agentic AI products; DevSecOps buyers at mid-to-large tech companies.

**Links**
- https://www.openappa.com/
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49867620
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49873303

## 2. 🔧 Local/Offline LLM Inference for Personal Projects

**Pain point:** Developers running personal AI projects struggle with cost, privacy, and reliability of cloud inference — they want local or cheap alternatives.

**Evidence**
- "Ask HN: How are you getting inference for personal projects?" (6 engagement)
- "Show HN: Kern Sandbox, run LLM-generated code locally, no cloud, no daemon" (3 engagement)
- "Show HN: I emulated Roborock's cloud so my vacuum works without internet" (3 engagement — signals broader offline-first frustration)
- **3 posts | ~12 total engagement | Specific recurring ask**

**Who pays:** Indie developers, privacy-conscious builders, hobbyists who hit API cost walls.

**Links**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49866739
- https://getkern.dev/guide/sandbox.html

## 3. 📊 AI Workflow Tooling for Developers

**Pain point:** Developers being pushed to use AI don't have structured, reproducible workflows — and management mandates are creating friction without good tooling.

**Evidence**
- "Ask HN: What does your AI workflow look like?" (7 engagement)
- "Management is forcing us to use AI" (24 engagement)
- "Show HN: I turned my team's coding-agent history into a knowledge graph" (3 engagement)
- "Using AI to develop software while being Anti-AI" (4 engagement)
- **4 posts | ~38 total engagement | Moderate specificity**

**Who pays:** Engineering managers and team leads at companies mandating AI adoption; dev tooling buyers.

**Links**
