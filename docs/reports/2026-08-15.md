# Validated Business Idea Clusters from HN

*Honest note upfront: This dataset is thin. Most posts lack excerpts, so signals come from titles alone. Only 3–4 clusters have real evidence. I'm not padding.*

---

## 🥇 1. HN/Tech News Curation & Filtering

**Pain point:** Developers are overwhelmed by HN's AI content flood and can't follow the signal they actually care about.

**Evidence**
- "How do you keep up with HN these days?" — 80 engagement
- "Move AI news from HN to somewhere else?" — 16 engagement
- **2 posts, 96 total engagement, very specific ask**

**Who'd pay:** Power HN users, senior devs, researchers — likely $5–15/mo for a filtered, personalized digest or alternative frontend with topic blocking.

**Comps gap:** TLDR/newsletters exist but don't offer granular source-level filtering with user control.

**Links**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49308059
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49295962

## 🥈 2. AI-Assisted Code Testing & Review

**Pain point:** Teams are uncertain whether AI-generated code still needs human review, and existing testing is not built for AI-authored codebases.

**Evidence**
- "Does a human still review your code?" — 21 engagement
- "Deltix – AI Driven Testing" (Show HN) — 32 engagement
- "Be honest: When did you last clean up obsolete code?" — 15 engagement
- **3 posts, 68 total engagement, product already attempting this**

**Who'd pay:** Engineering teams at mid-size companies shipping AI-assisted code — $50–500/mo seat pricing is plausible.

## 🥉 3. Structured LLM Context Management

**Pain point:** LLM chat conversations lose context and structure; users need a way to organize, branch, and edit the reasoning graph behind a conversation.

**Evidence**
- "ThoughtDAG – editable context graph for LLM conversations" — 14 engagement
- "Desktop automation stop lying to AI agents" — 3 engagement
- **2 posts, 17 total engagement, niche but technically specific**

**Who'd pay:** Power AI users, prompt engineers, researchers, technical writers — harder to monetize broadly but strong PLG potential with open-core model.

**Links**

## ⚠️ Honest Verdict

**Only clusters 1 and 2 have meaningful evidence.** Cluster 3 is a real problem space but thinly represented here. Cluster 4 is one founder's bet, not a validated pain.

**What's missing from this dataset that would change the analysis:** Post comments/excerpts. Title-only data from HN is noisy. The top signal (80 engagement on "how do you keep up with HN") is the clearest business-relevant pain point in the entire batch.
