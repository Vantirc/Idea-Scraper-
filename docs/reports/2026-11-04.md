# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Scrape

---

## 🥇 1. AI Privacy & Data Control Tools

**Pain point:** Users don't know (or can't control) when AI tools are training on their data, proxying their requests, or exposing credentials.

**Evidence strength:** 4 posts, ~505 engagement, highly specific complaints
- Gemini trains on chat history without clear consent (395 engagement — top post in dataset)
- Postman proxying API data without permission (74 engagement)
- Claude Code querying raw GitHub tokens on startup (3 engagement)
- Anthropic requiring government ID to sign up (25 engagement)

**What this looks like:** A privacy layer/proxy that sits between developers and AI tools — auditing what data leaves your machine, flagging policy changes, and enforcing data boundaries. Could also be a compliance dashboard for teams using AI tools.

**Who'd pay:** Security-conscious devs, enterprise teams, compliance officers, startups handling sensitive user data.

- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50360947
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50365442
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50356209

## 🥈 2. CAPTCHA Friction Relief for Developers & Power Users

**Pain point:** CAPTCHA systems are becoming so aggressive they block legitimate users constantly, with no good escape hatch.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, ~131 engagement, very specific frustration
- "Prove you are a human" hitting at every other website (126 engagement)
- Google reCAPTCHA requiring QR scan on mobile (5 engagement)

**What this looks like:** A browser extension or identity layer that pre-verifies humanity once and passes trusted signals to sites — think Cloudflare's one-click proof-of-personhood but portable and user-controlled. Or a B2B tool helping site owners tune CAPTCHA sensitivity to stop blocking real users.

**Who'd pay:** Power users / developers (browser extension), or website owners losing conversions to false CAPTCHA positives (B2B SaaS).

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50355959
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50358743

## 🥉 3. AI Agent Tool-Call Auditing

**Pain point:** When AI agents autonomously call external tools, there's no audit trail, permission gate, or accountability layer.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, ~6 engagement, niche but technically specific
- Shield KYA open-source audit trail for AI agent tool calls (3 engagement)
- Discussion thread on whether AI agents will/should pay for tools they use (3 engagement)

**What this looks like:** An middleware layer for agentic AI pipelines that logs, gates, and approves tool calls — with role-based permissions, cost controls, and compliance exports. Someone is already building the open-source version (Shield KYA); the opportunity is the managed/enterprise version.

**Who'd pay:** Teams running autonomous AI agents in production, compliance-heavy industries (finance, healthcare), platform builders.

**Sources:**
- https://github.com/The-Pixel-Boys/shield-kya
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50364183

## 4. Local LLM Setup & Hardware Advisory

**Pain point:** Developers want to run powerful LLMs locally (for privacy/cost) but navigating hardware options and setup is confusing.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, ~15 engagement, specific budget ask
- "Best local LLM options under $2500" (6 engagement)
- "Will cheap high-quality AI end this December?" — concern about access/cost changes (9 engagement)

**What this looks like:** A curated comparison tool (think PCPartPicker but for local AI rigs) or a managed local-LLM-in-a-box hardware product. Could also be a consulting/setup service.

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-focused developers, enterprises avoiding cloud AI, hobbyists and researchers.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50355440
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50353859

## 5. Developer Trust Layer for SaaS Tools

**Pain point:** Developers repeatedly discover that popular SaaS tools (Postman, Gitbook, etc.) are breached or mishandling data, with no early warning system.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, ~274 engagement, reactive but high signal
- Gitbook breached (200 engagement)
- Postman proxying data (74 engagement — see also cluster #1)

**What this looks like:** A monitoring service that tracks data handling policy changes, breach reports, and security advisories for SaaS tools in a team's stack — with Slack/email alerts and risk scoring. Similar to Socket.dev but for SaaS policies, not npm packages.

**Who'd pay:** CTOs, security teams, DevOps leads at companies with strict vendor risk policies.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50354336
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50360947

## ⚠️ Honest Caveat

**Clusters 1 and 5 overlap** (both driven by SaaS data trust). Combined, that's the **strongest single theme in this dataset** — ~779 total engagement across privacy/security breach posts. If you're picking one idea to investigate, that's the one.

Clusters 3 and 4 have **low engagement** and are early-signal at best. Validate before building.

Posts dropped as noise: GitHub/ChatGPT outage posts, election sentiment, "first time feeling optimistic," Google Flights business model, Steve Yegge keynote excitement, game/toy Show HNs with <5 engagement.
