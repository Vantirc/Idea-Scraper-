# Business Idea Clusters from HN Data

> **Honest upfront note:** This dataset is thin. Most posts have very low engagement (2–6 comments) and no excerpts to validate depth of pain. Only **4 clusters** have meaningful signal. I'm not padding to 8.

---

## 1. AI Skill Development for Junior Devs

**Pain point:** Junior developers can't build real coding intuition when AI writes all their code for them, and teams don't know how to structure AI-assisted workflows that still promote growth.

**Evidence strength:**
- 2 directly related posts
- Combined engagement: 21
- Moderately specific asks ("how do PR reviews work now?", "how does a junior dev get better?")

**Who'd pay:** Engineering managers, bootcamps, developer education platforms, L&D teams at tech companies

**Sources:**
- [How can a junior dev get better when they have to use AI?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49193519) (15)
- [Ask HN: How do you do PR reviews now?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49195510) (6)

---

## 2. AI Rollout Playbook for Non-Technical Teams

**Pain point:** Companies with mostly non-technical employees have no proven playbook for adopting AI tools at scale without chaos or low adoption.

**Evidence strength:**
- 1 post, but highly specific and practical ask
- Engagement: 3
- Very targeted question ("100 employees, mostly non-technical — what worked?")

**Who'd pay:** HR/L&D leaders, operations consultants, SMB owners, internal IT teams at mid-size companies

**Sources:**
- [AI rollout for 100 employees, mostly non-technical](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49197894) (3)

---

## 3. Investing/Trading Community with HN-Style Curation

**Pain point:** There's no high-quality, signal-rich community forum for serious investing and trading discussion — Reddit is noisy, Bloomberg is paywalled, and HN doesn't cover it.

**Evidence strength:**
- 1 post, engagement: 11
- Explicit "does this exist?" ask — strong product request signal
- Validated gap: person already searched and couldn't find it

**Who'd pay:** Retail traders, quant hobbyists, finance professionals who hate Bloomberg Terminal UX, fintech startups wanting engaged audiences

**Sources:**
- [Is there any HN but for investing/trading/economics?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49201546) (11)

---

## 4. AI-Assisted Medical/Appointment Pre-Rehearsal Tool

**Pain point:** People use AI to prepare for doctor or dentist visits — role-playing conversations, understanding terminology, knowing what to ask — but no dedicated tool exists for this.

**Evidence strength:**
- 1 post, engagement: 6
- The question itself reveals an already-happening behavior (not a wish, an observation)
- Strong consumer health angle

**Who'd pay:** Health-anxious consumers, chronic illness patients, elderly users (via family), telehealth platforms as a feature

**Sources:**
- [Do you rehearse with AI before seeing a doctor or dentist?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49196112) (6)

---

## ⚠️ Bottom Line

The dataset is **low-signal overall** — no Reddit posts, no excerpts, mostly single-digit engagement. Clusters 1 and 3 are the strongest bets. Treat this as directional, not validated. Run these titles through Reddit search, Exploding Topics, or a targeted HN thread before building anything.
