# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Scrape

---

## Honest Assessment First

This batch is **thin on evidence**. Most posts are low-engagement noise, speculation, or venting. Only **3–4 clusters** have genuine signal worth noting. I'm not padding to 8.

---

## Cluster 1: PR Culture & Code Review Friction
**Idea Name:** Structured Code Review Standards Tool

**Pain Point:** New engineers get blindsided by unwritten, subjective code review standards that vary by reviewer, causing friction and failed PRs.

**Evidence:**
- 1 post, but **52 engagement** (highest in the dataset by a wide margin)
- Specific, relatable scenario ("one month in, PR rejected")
- A perennial HN topic with known depth of feeling

**Who'd Pay:** Engineering managers, dev-tools companies, mid-size teams onboarding frequently (10–500 eng orgs). Think Loom for code review norms, or a Notion template layer on top of GitHub PRs.

**Sources:**
- [Coworker rejects PR (52 eng)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50622809)

---

## Cluster 2: Security Breach Blast Radius Limiting
**Idea Name:** Personal/SMB Breach Damage Minimization Layer

**Pain Point:** Everyone assumes they *will* be breached — but there's no simple, opinionated tooling to minimize damage when it happens.

**Evidence:**
- 1 post, **17 engagement**
- Question is specific and actionable ("how do you limit blast radius?")
- Complements the Amazon hack curiosity post (+5 eng)
- Growing anxiety around inevitable breaches vs. prevention-only security tools

**Who'd Pay:** SMBs, security-conscious developers, IT managers who can't afford enterprise SIEM tools. A "breach readiness checklist + monitoring" SaaS at $20–$100/mo.

**Sources:**
- [Limit the blast radius (17 eng)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50626772)
- [Did Amazon get hacked? (5 eng)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50615815)

---

## Cluster 3: Deep Work & Slack Interruption Management
**Idea Name:** Focus Shield / Async-First Workspace Tool

**Pain Point:** Slack interruptions constantly break deep work, and developers have no lightweight way to signal or enforce focus time across a team.

**Evidence:**
- 1 post, only **3 engagement** — low
- BUT: this is a known, documented pain with existing competitors (Reclaim.ai, Clockwise, Status Hero) — market validation exists *outside* this dataset
- The fact someone built something and posted it suggests founder-market fit signal

**Who'd Pay:** Remote-first engineering teams, knowledge workers, team leads. SaaS $8–$15/user/mo.

**Sources:**
- [Handle Slack interruptions (3 eng)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50630534)

---

## Cluster 4: macOS Email Client Gap
**Idea Name:** A Actually Good macOS Email Client

**Pain Point:** In 2026, there is still no email client for macOS that satisfies power users.

**Evidence:**
- 1 post, **9 engagement**
- Low engagement *here*, but this complaint surfaces on HN every 12–18 months with hundreds of comments historically
- Treat this as a **recurring signal** not a one-off

**Who'd Pay:** Mac power users, indie professionals, anyone paying for Superhuman ($30/mo) but wanting a native experience. Clear willingness-to-pay precedent exists.

**Sources:**
- [No good macOS email client (9 eng)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50630985)
