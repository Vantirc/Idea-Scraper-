# Validated Business Ideas from Reddit/HN Scrape

---

## 🥇 1. Senior Tech Unemployment / Career Recovery

**Pain point:** Experienced engineers (10–15+ years) are struggling to find work for extended periods, suggesting broken job-search tooling, signal-to-noise failures in hiring, or age/AI displacement bias.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, **998 total engagement** (709 + 289 — the two highest-engagement items in the entire dataset). The immigration AMA is closely related: people are exploring visa/legal options as career pivots or to retain talent. Extremely high engagement = real, widespread pain.

**Who'd pay:** Senior engineers in job search, recruiters targeting experienced hires, immigration attorneys, outplacement firms, career coaching platforms.

**Opportunity angles:**
- Senior-focused job board with signal filtering
- Career audit / repositioning coaching tool
- Immigration pathway advisor for tech workers

**Sources:**
- [Ask HN: Unemployed 2y, 15 years experience](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50583761) — 709 pts
- [Immigration attorney AMA (YC/startups)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50577613) — 289 pts

## 🥈 2. AI Agent Safety & Security Tooling

**Pain point:** Developers deploying AI coding agents have no reliable way to audit what those agents are actually executing — and the risk of destructive actions (e.g., `rm -rf`) or prompt leaks is real and unaddressed.

**Evidence strength:** 5 posts, **~52 total engagement**. Multiple independent builders are shipping tools in this exact space simultaneously — a strong "category forming" signal.

**Who'd pay:** DevOps/platform teams, AI-forward engineering orgs, security-conscious startups using Claude/Cursor/Codex agents.

**Opportunity angles:**
- Runtime agent audit/scanner (SaaS or CLI)
- Policy-as-code layer for agent permissions
- Prompt privacy/leakage detection middleware

**Sources:**
- [ClawVet – AST scanner & rug-pull diff for AI agent skills](https://clawvet.vercel.app/) — 6 pts
- [Pantry – open source scanner for coding agent actions](https://waffuru.ai/products/pantry) — 5 pts
- [RMRFBench – how often AI agents wreck servers](https://www.rmrfbench.com/) — 4 pts
- [OpenRouter/Minimax prompt leak disclosure](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50585962) — 31 pts
- [Shared memory & rules plugin for agents](https://github.com/keepplain/keepplain-plugin) — 6 pts

## 🥉 3. Authentic Job Applications vs. AI Spam

**Pain point:** The job market is being flooded with AI-generated resumes and applications, making it harder for real candidates to be seen and for employers to filter signal from noise.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, **~10 engagement** — low raw numbers but the "Show HN" product launch + the "SWE is dying" anxiety thread together reflect a clear market reaction to AI application inflation.

**Who'd pay:** Job seekers who want to stand out authentically, HR/recruiting teams drowning in AI-generated applications, ATS vendors.

**Opportunity angles:**
- "Human-verified" application credential/badge
- Employer-side AI-application filter
- Anti-AI-resume coaching or rewriting service

**Sources:**
- [Stop applying to jobs using AI-generated resume](https://unmark.grandimam.com/) — 7 pts
- [Ask HN: Was I proven correct, SWE is dying?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50580476) — 3 pts

## 4. Local/Offline AI Hardware & Infrastructure

**Pain point:** Developers want to run AI models locally for cost, privacy, or latency reasons but lack clear guidance on hardware selection and setup.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, **~10 engagement**. Niche but highly specific asks from a technically sophisticated, purchase-ready audience.

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-conscious developers, enterprise IT buyers, prosumer AI hobbyists.

**Opportunity angles:**
- Hardware recommendation engine / configurator for local AI
- Managed local LLM setup service
- Pre-built local AI appliance

**Sources:**
- [Ask HN: Did you buy hardware for Local AI?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50578897) — 4 pts
- [Ask HN: What is your current hardware setup?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50587796) — 5 pts

## 5. Open-Source Scrivener / Writing Tool Alternative

**Pain point:** Writers want serious long-form writing software that assists structurally without AI ghostwriting their content — Scrivener is dated, and AI writing tools go too far.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 6 engagement — weak numerically, but the product framing ("AI that never writes") is a sharp, differentiated positioning that signals real user demand that existing tools are missing.

**Who'd pay:** Authors, screenwriters, journalists, academics who want organizational help without AI content generation.

**Sources:**
- [Musset – open-source Scrivener alternative](https://github.com/Musset-App/musset) — 6 pts

## ⚠️ Honest Caveat

**Clusters 1 and 2 have genuine evidence.** Cluster 1 especially — nearly 1,000 engagement points on senior unemployment is a screaming signal. Cluster 2 shows organic market formation with 5 independent builders converging on the same problem.

**Clusters 3–5 are plausible but thin.** Treat them as early directional signals, not validated demand. Would need Reddit/forum corroboration before building.

**Dropped as noise:** GTA 6 reviews, Riemann Hypothesis discussion, Rust/Zig/C debates, HN meta-discussion, orbital compute speculation, Uber ratings decay — none translate to actionable business opportunities.
