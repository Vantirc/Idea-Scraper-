# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Scrape

**Honest upfront note:** This dataset is thin. Most posts have low engagement (2–10), no excerpts, and many are news reactions rather than pain-point signals. Only **3 clusters** have meaningful evidence. I'm not padding to 8.

---

## 🥇 Cluster 1: AI Agent Infrastructure & Orchestration

**Idea name:** Agent Scheduling, Payments & Governance Tools

**Pain point:** Developers building multi-agent AI systems have no standardized way to schedule, fund, or govern agent-to-agent interactions.

**Evidence:**
- `Moadim.io` – scheduler for agents (36 engagement — highest in dataset)
- `OpenSpender` – agent-to-agent payments (8 engagement)
- `Covenant` – governance framework for multi-agent systems (3 engagement)
- 3 posts, ~47 total engagement, all pointing at the same infrastructure gap

**Specificity:** High — these are builders shipping products, not just complaining

**Who'd pay:** Dev teams deploying autonomous agents in production; AI-first startups; enterprises piloting agentic workflows

**Sources:**
- https://moadim.io/
- https://openspender.com/agent-to-agent
- https://github.com/asalsali/covenant-framework-community

## 🥈 Cluster 2: AI Tool Reliability & Unpredictable Access

**Idea name:** AI Provider Monitoring / Reliability Layer

**Pain point:** Developers can't depend on AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI) due to silent setting changes, random usage resets, and unexpected outages — with no proactive alerts.

**Evidence:**
- "Check your Claude settings — silently enabled remote access" (11 engagement)
- "Anthropic just reset usage, including Fable" (11 engagement)
- "Two random Anthropic allocation resets this week" (5 engagement)
- 3 posts, ~27 total engagement, recurring frustration pattern

**Specificity:** Medium — the complaints are specific but no one has explicitly asked for a solution tool

**Who'd pay:** Developers and small teams with production workflows depending on Claude/GPT; AI-heavy SaaS companies

**Potential product:** A monitoring/alerting service that watches AI provider status, usage limits, and setting changes — like Better Uptime but for AI APIs

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49565799
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49570522
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49570445

## 🥉 Cluster 3: ISP-Level Silent Content Blocking

**Idea name:** ISP DNS Transparency / Bypass Tool

**Pain point:** ISPs like Xfinity silently block newly registered or unpopular domains, breaking legitimate services with zero user notification.

**Evidence:**
- "Xfinity silently blocks lots of new domains" (16 engagement)
- "Any alternatives to Archive.ph & co?" (2 engagement) — adjacent signal
- 2 posts, ~18 engagement

**Specificity:** Medium — clear technical problem, implicit demand for transparency or workaround tooling

**Who'd pay:** Small business owners whose domains get blocked; developers testing new services; privacy-conscious consumers (harder to monetize); could be a B2B DNS/network product

**Potential product:** A domain health checker that tests reachability across major ISP DNS resolvers, with alerts when your domain gets silently blocked

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49568385
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49574822
