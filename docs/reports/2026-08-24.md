## Honest Upfront Assessment

## 🥇 1. AI Coding Agent Reliability & Alternatives

**Pain point:** Developers depend on AI coding agents that break or disappear, leaving them stranded with no clear migration path.

**Evidence**
- "Continue coding agent is dead. Alternatives?" (engagement: 6)
- "Show HN: Ever Wanted to Call Codex from Claude Code? My Harness Orchestrator" (engagement: 3)
- "Show HN: Make ordinary subagent workflows persistent and trackable" (engagement: 3)

Posts: 3 | Total engagement: 12 | Specificity: **High** — explicit tool failure + two independent builders solving orchestration/persistence

**Who pays:** Individual developers and small engineering teams paying $20–50/mo for Copilot/Cursor-tier tools who can't afford workflow disruption.

**Sources**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49398366
- https://github.com/ptmrio/harness-subagent
- https://github.com/ringlochid/oh-my-subagents

## 🥈 2. Video/Audio Transcript Extraction & Learning Tools

**Pain point:** People want to read, search, and take structured notes from YouTube and TED content without manual effort.

**Evidence**
- "Ask HN: Anyone set up ways to easily obtain and read transcripts from Ted, YT?" (engagement: 6)
- "I built a tool to generate Cornell notes from YouTube videos" (engagement: 6)

Posts: 2 | Total engagement: 12 | Specificity: **High** — one is an explicit ask, one is a direct builder response to the same need

**Who pays:** Students, researchers, knowledge workers, lifelong learners. Clear willingness to pay in the productivity/learning tools category (Readwise, Notion users).

**Sources**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49405684
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49406926

## 5. AI for Practical 3D/Physical Object Design

**Pain point:** AI can generate art and code fluently but fails at simple real-world physical designs (e.g., a custom ramp for a robot vacuum).

**Evidence**
- "Why can AI generate Super Mario but not a wedge ramp for my robot vacuum?" (engagement: 16)

Posts: 1 | Total engagement: 16 | Specificity: **Medium** — points to a real gap but framed as commentary, not an explicit product ask

**Who pays:** Hardware tinkerers, makers, small manufacturers needing parametric/CAD generation. Longer path to monetization than the clusters above.

**Sources**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49405520
