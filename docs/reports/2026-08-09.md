# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Scan

---

## Honest Upfront Note
This dataset is thin. Most posts have no excerpts, so signals come from titles only. Engagement numbers are low across the board. I found **4 clusters with real (if weak) evidence** and will not pad beyond that.

---

## 1. 🤖 AI Coding Agent Tooling & Deployment

**Pain point:** Developers can write code with AI agents but struggle to deploy, manage token costs, and resume sessions across different agent tools.

**Evidence:**
- 4 posts, ~36 combined engagement
- Specific and repeated asks — not vague

**Posts:**
- [How do you go from writing code to deploying with agents?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49227024) — 17 eng
- [How do you use Claude Code / Codex at work for enterprise?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49228883) — 3 eng
- [Tura – 80% less tokens, better results](https://github.com/Tura-AI/tura) — 7 eng
- [agent-hop – resume sessions across any agent](https://github.com/hetpatel-11/agent-hop) — 3 eng
- [Benzi – coding harness beating Claude Code](https://benzi.fly.dev/about) — 8 eng

**Who'd pay:** Engineering teams using AI coding assistants at scale; dev tool companies. Mid-market SaaS ($50–200/mo per seat).

---

## 2. 📱 Social Network Design Gaps

**Pain point:** People are actively unhappy with existing social networks and speculating about what a better one would look like — a recurring signal of unmet needs.

**Evidence:**
- 2 posts, ~86 combined engagement
- Highest single-post engagement in dataset (55)
- Ask HN format = high intent to discuss specifics

**Posts:**
- [If a social network were created today, what would you want?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49226693) — 55 eng
- [Is Ask HN shrinking?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49221476) — 31 eng (meta-commentary on community decay)

**Who'd pay:** Hard consumer B2C problem, but niche community platforms (think Discourse, Farcaster-style) could monetize via SaaS seats or community subscriptions.

**Caveat:** "Build a better social network" is a graveyard. Only viable as a niche vertical community tool.

---

## 3. 🔍 LLM Transparency & Training Data Visibility

**Pain point:** Developers and researchers can't tell what data specific LLMs were trained on, making model selection and compliance decisions difficult.

**Evidence:**
- 1 post, 5 engagement — low but very specific ask
- Growing regulatory pressure (EU AI Act) makes this commercially timely

**Posts:**
- [How can I tell which LLMs have been trained on what?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49218466) — 5 eng

**Who'd pay:** Enterprise legal/compliance teams, AI procurement officers, regulated industries (finance, healthcare). B2B SaaS, $500–2k/mo range.

**Caveat:** Single post. Needs external validation before building.

---

## 4. 📲 App Store Link Workarounds for Social Platforms

**Pain point:** Instagram blocks direct App Store links in bios, forcing app developers to lose installs or use clunky workarounds.

**Evidence:**
- 1 post, 3 engagement — but someone already built and shipped a solution
- Pain is specific, proven, and affects every indie app developer with an Instagram presence

**Posts:**
- [AppPage – Fix Instagram blocking App Store links in bio](https://apppage.co/) — 3 eng

**Who'd pay:** Indie app developers, mobile growth marketers. Low-cost tool ($10–30/mo). High volume potential given the size of the app dev market.

## Bottom Line

**Strongest bet:** AI agent deployment/session management (#1) — clearest repeated pain with people already attempting solutions. **Dark horse:** LLM training data transparency (#3) — low engagement now but regulatory tailwinds are real.
