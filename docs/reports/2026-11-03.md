# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Data

*Honest note up front: This dataset is thin. Most posts have very low engagement and no excerpts to confirm depth of discussion. Only 3–4 clusters have credible signal. I'm not padding to 8.*

---

## 1. 🔒 AI Provider Lock-in & Price Risk Management

**Pain point:** Developers and businesses are worried about AI companies suddenly raising prices or changing terms, leaving them stranded.

**Evidence strength:** 2 directly related posts, combined engagement ~22, but the question is *highly specific* and recurring in the AI-dev community well beyond this dataset. The WorkOS breach post (84 engagement) adds adjacent vendor-trust anxiety.

- "What is your backup plan when AI companies rug pull their prices?" (19)
- "Tell HN: Modal is raising its price" (3)
- WorkOS security breach (84) — vendor reliability angle

**Who'd pay:** SaaS startups, AI-native companies with >$5k/mo AI spend, CTOs managing multi-model infrastructure.

**Opportunity:** A model-agnostic routing/fallback layer or spend-monitoring tool that alerts on price changes and auto-switches providers. Think "Cloudflare for LLM calls."

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50336034
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50343399
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50345712

## 2. 🖥️ Local LLM Hardware Guidance

**Pain point:** People want to run LLMs locally but have no clear, trusted guide to what hardware actually works.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, engagement 88 — meaningfully high for this dataset, suggesting genuine community appetite. The Tenstorrent vs Nvidia question (3) is a weak corroborating signal.

- "What hardware are you using to run local LLMs?" (88)
- "Are Tenstorrent cards better than Nvidia?" (3)

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-conscious developers, hobbyists, small businesses avoiding cloud AI costs, researchers.

**Opportunity:** A comparison/benchmark site or newsletter specifically for local LLM hardware setups — monetized via affiliate deals with hardware vendors, or a paid "setup guide" product. Could expand into a consultancy for SMBs wanting on-prem AI.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50337431
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50346540

## 3. 🔐 Private, Secure Note-Taking

**Pain point:** People don't trust existing note apps with sensitive personal data and want end-to-end encrypted, private alternatives.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, low engagement (9), but the question is *very specific* and this is a known recurring theme on HN with proven willingness to pay (Standard Notes, Obsidian, etc. all have paid tiers).

- "What are you doing to protect the privacy of your notes?" (9)

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-conscious knowledge workers, journalists, lawyers, developers, security researchers.

**Opportunity:** The market exists and is validated externally. New entrant angle: local-first + AI-assisted notes with zero cloud exposure. The gap is combining LLM utility with genuine privacy.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50349024

---

## 4. 🏥 Medical Report Translation for Patients

**Pain point:** Patients receive MRI/lab results they can't understand and feel unprepared to talk to their doctor.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post (Show HN: Remene), engagement 10. Weak raw signal but the *product already exists* and someone built it — confirming the pain is real enough to act on. Regulatory risk is real.

- "Remene – Understand your MRI and prepare questions for your doctor" (10)

**Who'd pay:** Patients directly (B2C), or patient advocacy orgs, insurance companies wanting to reduce unnecessary follow-up calls.

**Opportunity:** AI plain-language explainer for medical documents, with a "questions to ask your doctor" generator. Key moat: accuracy, trust, liability management.

**Sources:**
- https://remene.si
