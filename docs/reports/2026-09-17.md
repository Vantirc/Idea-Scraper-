# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Scrape

---

## Honest Assessment Upfront

This is a **weak signal dataset**. Most posts have very low engagement (2–6 comments), no excerpts to analyze, and many are noise. Only **2–3 clusters** have meaningful evidence. I'm not padding to 8.

---

## Cluster 1: AI Agent Reliability & Trust
**"How do I know if my AI agent actually did it right?"**

**Pain point:** Developers don't have good tools to verify, measure, or trust the output of coding agents and AI pipelines — forcing awkward manual checks and repeated runs.

**Evidence:**
- *"How many runs before you trust a coding agent's result?"* (eng: 2)
- *"Has anyone measured how often agents use the skills you ship?"* (eng: 3)
- *"LLM based CI pipeline code generator"* (eng: 6)
- *"What is your approach to sensitive data in LLM prompts and traces?"* (eng: 3)

Total engagement: ~14 across 4 related posts. Low absolute numbers but **notably specific asks** — not venting, genuine methodology gaps.

**Who'd pay:** Dev teams shipping AI agents in production; ML platform teams at mid-size companies.

**Opportunity:** Observability/eval layer for agents — think Datadog but for agent behavior, skill usage, and output confidence.

## Cluster 2: Rogue Bot / Crawler Cost Protection
**"A bot destroyed my infrastructure bill and I had no recourse"**

**Pain point:** Third-party bots (AI crawlers, scrapers) are hitting hosted apps and generating massive unexpected cloud bills with no easy way to block or bill back the offender.

**Evidence:**
- *"Zuck's bot increased our Vercel bill 10x"* (eng: 10)
- *"How is AI destruction of books legal?"* (eng: 4) — adjacent IP/cost harm angle

Total engagement: ~14. The Vercel post is **highly specific and actionable** — a real incident with a named offender.

**Who'd pay:** Indie developers and small teams on usage-based hosting (Vercel, Netlify, Railway). This is a growing problem as AI crawlers multiply.

**Opportunity:** Bot firewall specifically designed for usage-based hosting platforms — blocks known AI crawlers, rate-limits by bot fingerprint, alerts on anomalous spend. Could integrate directly with Vercel/Netlify APIs.

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49732873

---

## Cluster 3: Google Business Profile Suspensions
**"Google suspended my business profile and there's no human to talk to"**

**Pain point:** Small businesses get their Google Business Profile suspended arbitrarily, losing local search visibility with no clear appeals process or support channel.

**Evidence:**
- *"Google has suspended our business profile"* (eng: 10)

Total engagement: 10. **Single post**, but this is a well-documented chronic problem (search any SEO forum). HN post confirms it's hitting technical founders too, not just non-tech SMBs.

**Who'd pay:** SMBs and local service businesses dependent on Google Maps/Search. Existing players like Yext are expensive and clunky.

**Opportunity:** Suspension monitoring + guided reinstatement service. Productized consulting with a software layer that monitors profile health, flags risk signals, and guides appeals. $50–200/mo easily justifiable if it protects local search revenue.

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49730532

## Bottom Line

**Only 3 clusters survived** with real signal. Cluster 2 (bot cost damage) is arguably the most actionable — it's a **new problem** (AI crawlers are recent), growing fast, and the victim is easy to reach (developers already on Vercel/HN). Cluster 3 is the most **monetizable** immediately since SMBs with lost Google profiles are in acute pain and will pay quickly.
