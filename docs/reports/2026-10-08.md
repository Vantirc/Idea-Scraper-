# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Scrape

---

## ⚠️ Honest Assessment First

Most posts here are low-engagement opinion threads, not specific pain signals. Only **3–4 clusters** have genuine business evidence. I'm not padding the rest.

---

## 🥇 #1 — AI Code Security Review Tool

**Pain point:** Developers don't know which AI model to trust for security audits, and no purpose-built tool exists.

**Evidence**
- 2 posts, ~75 combined engagement
- "Ask HN: Which frontier model can do code security reviews" (12 engagement)
- "Tell HN: Amazon forgot how to write and test code" (14 engagement)
- Specific, technical ask — not vague complaining

**Who'd pay:** Security-conscious dev teams, CTOs at startups post-launch, regulated industries (fintech, healthtech)

**Estimated willingness to pay:** $50–200/mo per team

**Links**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49996594
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49997864

---

## 🥈 #2 — AI Cost Attribution for Client Billing

**Pain point:** Freelancers and agencies using AI coding tools (Claude Code, Codex) have no way to track and bill AI API costs per client.

**Evidence**
- 1 direct Show HN product already attempting this (Tallyhook)
- Existence of a builder signals real demand
- Niche but very specific and recurring pain

**Who'd pay:** Dev agencies, consultants billing hourly, any freelancer using AI tools on multiple client projects

**Estimated willingness to pay:** $20–50/mo

**Links**
- https://github.com/tallyhook/tallyhook-collector

---

## 🥉 #3 — Invisible Watermarking for AI-Generated Content

**Pain point:** Creators and platforms need a way to prove ownership or trace leaks of images, PDFs, and videos — especially as AI-generated content floods the web.

**Evidence**
- 1 Show HN (Etchv) — someone already building this
- Dovetails with high-engagement AI fingerprinting concern (Chrome AI API post, 6 engagement)
- Growing regulatory pressure on AI content provenance

**Who'd pay:** Media companies, legal teams, stock photo platforms, enterprises worried about document leaks

**Links**
- https://etchv.com
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49990335

---

## 🔍 #4 — AI Tax Filing Assistant (Weak Signal, Watch It)

**Pain point:** Tax filing software (TurboTax etc.) is painful and expensive, yet AI hasn't displaced it — people are asking why not.

**Evidence**
- 1 post, only 10 engagement
- Question framing suggests frustration, not a tested solution
- Regulatory complexity is the real blocker (not lack of interest)

**Who'd pay:** Self-employed, freelancers, small business owners

**⚠️ Caution:** This is a "why hasn't someone done X" question, not a validated gap. Regulatory and liability barriers are steep. Watch for more signal before building.

**Links**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50004396
