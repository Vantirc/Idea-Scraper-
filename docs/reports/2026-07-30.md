## 🥇 #1 — Domain/Hosting Provider Trust & Billing Protection

**Pain point:** Registrars and hosting providers can unexpectedly charge, lock, or rug-pull customers with no recourse.

**Evidence strength:**
- 1 post, but **121 engagement** — the highest in the entire dataset by a wide margin
- Very specific incident ($3,000 charge), resonates broadly
- Title says "[fixed]" meaning community helped — implies others have same fear

**Who'd pay:** SMBs, indie devs, anyone running production infrastructure on rented domains/hosting

**Opportunity signals:**
- Domain/hosting bill monitoring & anomaly alerts
- Escrow-style registrar or multi-registrar redundancy service
- "Domain insurance" or switchover automation

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49099021

## 🥈 #2 — AI Code Risk in Production

**Pain point:** Developers are shipping AI-generated code without clear frameworks for managing quality, security, or production failure risk.

**Evidence strength:**
- 2 directly relevant posts, combined ~21 engagement
- Specific, actionable asks ("how are you handling," "do models train on slop")
- Recurring theme across multiple angles: output quality, training contamination, review gaps

**Who'd pay:** Engineering teams at startups and mid-size companies; CTOs nervous about liability

**Opportunity signals:**
- AI code audit / static analysis layer tuned for LLM-generated patterns
- Pre-merge risk scoring for AI-assisted PRs
- Policy/compliance tooling for "AI code provenance"

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49107587
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49105219

## 🥉 #3 — Privacy-First Analytics

**Pain point:** Developers want website analytics that don't compromise user privacy or violate regulations like GDPR.

**Evidence strength:**
- 1 post, 9 engagement — low but **extremely specific ask** ("what do you use for privacy focused analytics")
- This is a known recurring question on HN; validated market with existing players (Plausible, Fathom) proving willingness to pay

**Who'd pay:** Indie developers, EU-based startups, any site needing GDPR compliance

**Opportunity signals:**
- Niche: privacy analytics for specific verticals (healthcare, fintech) where existing tools are too generic
- Bundled consent + analytics product

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49103122

## 🏅 #4 — AI-Assisted Developer Learning / Onboarding

**Pain point:** Developers don't have a clear, structured path to learn AI-assisted development workflows from scratch.

**Evidence strength:**
- 2 loosely related posts, combined ~20 engagement
- Specific ask format ("how would you learn from the ground up") signals real personal need, not just curiosity
- Adjacent post on LLM coding productivity confirms the theme

**Who'd pay:** Junior-to-mid devs; bootcamps; companies reskilling engineering teams

**Opportunity signals:**
- Structured course or cohort program specifically on AI-native development
- "AI dev workflow" audit + coaching for teams

**Links:**
