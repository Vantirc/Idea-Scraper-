## 🥇 Cluster 1: AI Model Usage Compliance & Distillation Defense

**Idea name:** AI ToS Compliance & Audit Tool

**Pain point:** Companies using AI APIs have no way to prove they're *not* violating terms of service (e.g., model distillation), leaving them legally exposed with no defense.

**Evidence strength:**
- 1 post, but **735 engagement** — by far the highest in the dataset
- Extremely specific, real legal/business threat
- The question itself *is* the product gap: there's no existing tool to audit or prove compliance

**Who'd pay:** Any startup or company calling LLM APIs at scale — especially those fine-tuning models or building AI products on top of frontier models. Legal/compliance teams at AI companies.

**Rough product shape:** Logging + attestation layer that records model usage patterns in a tamper-evident way, specifically designed to demonstrate you're *not* distilling. Think "audit trail for AI API calls."

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50630809

## 🥈 Cluster 2: Security Breach Blast Radius Limiting

**Idea name:** Breach Impact Minimization Platform

**Pain point:** Developers and security teams accept breaches as inevitable but lack practical tooling to minimize damage when they occur — not prevention, but *containment*.

**Evidence strength:**
- 2 posts (breach blast radius + CIA breach news), ~31 combined engagement
- Specific framing ("limit the blast radius") signals someone who knows what they want
- CIA breach adds timely real-world context

**Who'd pay:** Security-conscious engineering teams, mid-market SaaS companies, compliance-driven orgs (fintech, healthtech). This is adjacent to secrets management and zero-trust but more opinionated/tactical.

**Rough product shape:** Automated credential scoping audit + incident playbook generator. "If X is breached, here's exactly what's exposed and how to rotate/isolate it."

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50626772
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50639746

## 🥉 Cluster 3: macOS Email Client Gap

**Idea name:** Serious macOS Email Client

**Pain point:** In 2026, no email client on macOS satisfies power users — the gap between Apple Mail and clunky third-party options remains wide open.

**Evidence strength:**
- 1 post, 23 engagement
- The frustration is perennial (this exact complaint recurs every 2–3 years on HN)
- Low engagement here, but historically this is a *known recurring signal* on HN

**Who'd pay:** macOS power users, developers, founders. Likely $5–15/month. Superhuman proved willingness to pay at $30/month for email UX.

**Caveat:** This is a crowded, hard space. The fact it *keeps* being asked suggests it's genuinely unsolved, but also that it's genuinely hard to solve.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50630985
