# Business Idea Clusters from HN/Reddit Data

> **Honest caveat up front:** This dataset is thin. No Reddit posts, no excerpts, and most HN engagement numbers are very low. Only 3 clusters have meaningful signal. I'm not padding the rest.

---

## 🥇 #1 — LLM Cost & Billing Transparency

**Pain point:** Developers can't easily predict or understand what LLM API calls will cost before or after they're made.

**Evidence**
- "Ask HN: What Did Anthropic Bill Me For?" (engagement: 6)
- "Show HN: Slash-tokens – know LLM cost before the call leaves your machine" (engagement: 6)
- "Ask HN: Best LLM Subscription as of Now?" (engagement: 6)
- **Posts: 3 | Total engagement: 18 | Specificity: High** — two posts name the exact same problem from both sides (confusion after billing, prevention before billing)

**Who'd pay:** Individual developers and small teams using Claude/GPT APIs who get surprise bills. Also engineering leads at startups managing API budgets.

**Viable product:** Pre-flight token estimator + spend dashboard with per-model cost breakdowns and alerts.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49434551
- https://github.com/Wolfe-Jam/slash-tokens
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49433196

## 🥈 #2 — LLM Limitations & Quality Control

**Pain point:** LLMs have well-known, specific failure modes that users keep rediscovering — verbosity, reasoning gaps, unreliability — with no good tooling to detect or suppress them.

**Evidence**
- "Ask HN: What is one simple thing LLMs are insanely bad at?" (engagement: 94)
- "Ask HN: Why are Claude models so verbose?" (engagement: 6)
- "Show HN: Declaude" — a tool explicitly to reduce Claude verbosity (engagement: 8)
- "Is AI slowing you down?" (engagement: 24)
- **Posts: 4 | Total engagement: 132 | Specificity: Medium-High** — highest raw engagement in the dataset

**Who'd pay:** Power users and developers who rely on LLMs daily and lose time to output quality issues. Teams doing LLM evals.

**Viable product:** Output quality middleware — verbosity filter, hallucination flagging, structured output enforcer — as a lightweight proxy layer or SDK.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49443820
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49444272
- https://speak-english.tenken.co/
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49438590

## 🥉 #3 — System Design Documentation & Knowledge Transfer

**Pain point:** Engineering decisions made in rooms (or Slack threads) never get documented in a way junior engineers or future teammates can actually use.

**Evidence**
- "Ask HN: How do you explain system design to engineers who weren't in the room?" (engagement: 3)
- "Ask HN: How do you code review?" (engagement: 5)
- "Show HN: Agent skill to create HTML docs for planning" (engagement: 3)
- **Posts: 3 | Total engagement: 11 | Specificity: Medium** — small engagement but a perennial, known pain point in engineering orgs

**Who'd pay:** Engineering managers and CTOs at 20–200 person companies tired of tribal knowledge loss during hiring/onboarding.

**Viable product:** AI-assisted architecture decision record (ADR) tool that auto-generates docs from PR history, meeting notes, or prompts.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49444092
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49444984
- https://github.com/parthjshah95/visual-planning-skills
