# Validated Business Ideas from HN Data

> **Honest note up front:** This dataset is thin. Most posts have very low engagement (2–7 points), no excerpts, and many are philosophical/off-topic. Only **3–4 clusters** have genuine signal worth acting on. I'm not padding to 8.

---

## 🥇 1. Unified Bookmark / Save Manager Across Services

**Pain point:** People save content across dozens of platforms (Reddit, YouTube, Twitter, HN, Pocket, etc.) with no unified way to search or retrieve it.

**Evidence**
- `Ask HN: Any solution for bookmarks on various services?` — 7 engagement
- `Show HN: Save photo, video, and audio from websites in one step` — 7 engagement
- `Ask HN: How do you turn videos into searchable text without uploading them?` — 5 engagement
- **3 posts, ~19 total engagement, specific asks**

**Who'd pay:** Knowledge workers, researchers, heavy internet consumers, students. Raindrop.io and Readwise exist but clearly haven't solved this fully.

**Sources**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50019532
- https://menura.download
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50018918

---

## 🥈 2. AI That Learns Preferences, Not Just History

**Pain point:** Current AI/recommendation systems reflect what you *clicked*, not what you actually *like* — users want taste-aware AI.

**Evidence**
- `Can an AI learn what you like, rather than just what you've searched for?` — 5 engagement
- `Ask HN: Are you a subscribed LLM or a locally deployed open-source model?` — 28 engagement (signals active search for AI that fits *personal* needs)
- **2 posts, ~33 total engagement, conceptually specific**

**Who'd pay:** Consumers frustrated with Netflix/Spotify/YouTube recommendations. Also B2B angle: e-commerce personalization teams.

**Sources**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50017172
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50016644

---

## 🥉 3. Job Ad Bullshit Detector

**Pain point:** Job postings are padded with vague buzzwords and misleading signals — candidates can't tell real roles from HR noise.

**Evidence**
- `Show HN: Yeah Nah – a BS score for Australian job ads` — 15 engagement (highest of any "Show HN" product here)
- Engagement notably higher than most other Show HNs in this batch
- **1 post, 15 engagement, working prototype already exists**

**Who'd pay:** Job seekers (freemium), recruiters who want to write better ads, job boards wanting quality signals. Geographic expansion beyond AU is obvious.

**Sources**
- https://yeahnah.lol/

---

## 4. Shared Decision/Context Memory Across AI Tools

**Pain point:** Every AI tool starts from zero — decisions made in Claude aren't known by Cursor, ChatGPT, or your next session.

**Evidence**
- `Show HN: Rutter – shared decision memory across AI tools` — 3 engagement
- `Show HN: A simple to-do app for iPhone, Mac, and your agent` — 28 engagement
- Adjacent signal: high interest in local vs. subscribed LLMs (28 engagement) suggests people managing multiple AI setups
- **2–3 posts, ~34 engagement, but engagement is mostly on adjacent posts**

**Who'd pay:** Developers and power users running multi-agent or multi-tool workflows. Early but real market forming.

**Sources**
- https://ruttermemory.com/
- https://ilia.page/writing/introducing-nagare

## Bottom Line

**Real signal here is sparse.** The bookmark/save problem (#1) is the most validated with multiple independent posts. The job ad scorer (#3) is the most *proven* (working product, best Show HN engagement). The AI memory/preference clusters are early but directionally interesting given the broader AI tool proliferation trend.
