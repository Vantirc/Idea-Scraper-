# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Scrape

---

## 1. 🧑‍💻 AI-Era Developer Hiring & Assessment

**Pain point:** Companies don't know how to interview or evaluate developers now that AI can write code for them.

**Evidence strength:** 3 posts, 115 engagement, highly specific ask
- "How do you interview devs in a post-AI world?" (64)
- "So is AI taking our jobs?" (6)
- "As a freelancer how should I charge clients in AI era?" (5)

**Who'd pay:** HR tech companies, engineering managers, staffing/recruiting firms, freelance platforms

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49768826
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49758136
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49766918

## 2. 💳 Payment Processor Dispute & Protection

**Pain point:** Stripe and similar processors arbitrarily withhold large merchant balances with little recourse.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 28 engagement — but the topic is high-stakes and recurs constantly on HN historically. Signal is specific and financial.

**Who'd pay:** SaaS founders, e-commerce merchants, anyone processing >$10k/mo

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49764468

---

## 3. 🤖 Better Context Delivery to AI Agents

**Pain point:** Giving AI agents enough context (screenshots, state, UI) is tedious and repetitive — users want agents that "just know" what's on screen.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, 15 engagement, very specific frustration
- "I got tired of taking screenshots and explaining everything to AI agents" (7)
- "Show HN: WTF > Auto-check what your coding agent changed" (3)

**Who'd pay:** Developers using Cursor/Copilot/Claude daily, power users of AI coding tools

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49759108
- https://github.com/LinusInnovator/wtf

---

## 4. 📄 Fast PDF Generation (Non-Headless-Chrome)

**Pain point:** Generating PDFs via headless Chrome is slow and bloated — devs want a lightweight, sub-10ms alternative.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 13 engagement — but the solution is concrete, the market is real, and the Typst angle is novel.

**Who'd pay:** Backend developers, SaaS apps generating invoices/reports, document automation tools

**Links:**
- https://rubrol.com/

## 5. 📝 Offline-First Markdown Notes with Sync

**Pain point:** Developers want private, offline-capable note-taking with frictionless device sync — without cloud lock-in.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, 11 engagement — niche but recurring desire in dev communities
- "Set, offline Markdown note-taking app with device-to-device sync" (6)
- "What I Can Remember" (11, likely memory/notes-related)

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-conscious developers, researchers, writers who distrust cloud storage

**Links:**
- https://app.writewithset.com
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49762163

## ⚠️ Honest Assessment

**Clusters 3–5 are weak signals** — 1–2 posts each, low engagement, no Reddit corroboration. Treat them as *directions worth watching*, not validated pain points.

**Clusters 1 and 2 are the only ones with real evidence strength** from this dataset alone. The AI interviewing problem in particular has specificity, urgency, and a clear buyer. The Stripe withholding issue has financial stakes that reliably drive purchasing behavior.

**Recommend:** Cross-reference these clusters against Reddit subs (r/startups, r/ExperiencedDevs, r/cscareerquestions) before building anything.
