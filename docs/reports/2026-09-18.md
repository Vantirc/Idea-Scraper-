# Validated Business Idea Clusters

---

## 1. 🔐 MFA / Auth Recovery for Stolen Devices

**Pain point:** Losing access to Google Authenticator (and similar TOTP apps) after phone theft has no smooth recovery path — users are locked out of critical accounts.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, but 216 engagement — unusually high for a single Ask HN. The specificity of the question and volume of responses signals a widespread, recurring, high-stress problem.

**Who'd pay:** Individuals + SMBs who can't afford prolonged lockouts; IT/security teams managing employee access recovery.

**Opportunity angle:** A hardware-backed or cloud-synced auth recovery service with clear break-glass procedures — something between Authy's cloud sync and a proper enterprise recovery workflow, for the non-enterprise user.

**Source:** [Ask HN: How to recover Google auth after phone stolen?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49742976)

---

## 2. 🤖 AI Session Continuity / State Management

**Pain point:** AI coding sessions lose context silently — the model contradicts previous decisions, forgets project state, and there's no reliable way to carry structured context across sessions.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, ~6 engagement total — low raw numbers but both are unusually specific and solution-oriented, not just venting.

**Who'd pay:** Developers and teams using AI coding assistants (Cursor, Claude, Copilot) for multi-session projects; dev tool companies as an embedded feature.

**Opportunity angle:** A lightweight project-state layer (rules, decisions, architecture notes) that AI tools must respect — essentially a `.cursorrules` on steroids with version control and contradiction detection.

**Sources:**
- [Show HN: Continuity – project state your AI sessions can't silently contradict](https://github.com/vivcena01/ai-continuity-plugin)
- [Novgraph: Knowledge graph for codebases that saves 99% tokens](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49745176)

---

## 3. 🛡️ Sensitive Data Handling in LLM Pipelines

**Pain point:** Developers have no clear, practical approach to preventing sensitive data (PII, secrets, credentials) from leaking into LLM prompts, logs, and traces.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 3 engagement — low volume but extremely specific and actionable ask from practitioners, not curious observers. HIPAA/AI post adds adjacent signal (8 engagement).

**Who'd pay:** Enterprises in regulated industries (healthcare, finance, legal); any dev team running LLM-powered features at scale; compliance officers.

**Opportunity angle:** A middleware/SDK that scrubs, masks, or tokenizes sensitive fields before they reach any LLM API, with audit logs — essentially a data firewall for AI pipelines.

**Sources:**
- [Ask HN: What is your approach to sensitive data in LLM prompts and traces?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49738870)
- [Ask HN: HIPAA for AI vs. Licensed Providers](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49742520)

---

## 4. 🌐 Self-Hosted App Discovery & Management

**Pain point:** People want to self-host daily-use apps but discovery, setup, and maintenance remain fragmented and underdocumented.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 13 engagement — modest but the "daily use" framing indicates genuine adoption intent, not hobbyist curiosity.

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-conscious prosumers; small teams wanting to escape SaaS costs; homelab enthusiasts willing to pay for managed self-hosting orchestration.

**Opportunity angle:** A curated marketplace + one-click deployment layer for vetted self-hosted apps (think Coolify meets a recommendations engine), with managed updates.

**Source:** [Ask HN: What self-hosted apps do you use daily?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49746527)

---

## 5. 📁 Cross-Platform File Transfer to Restricted Devices

**Pain point:** Sending files from macOS to devices with no standard receive mechanism (air-gapped systems, locked-down hardware) is a persistent friction point.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 4 engagement — niche but someone built a tool for it (PortButler), which is the strongest possible signal that the pain is real enough to ship code over.

**Who'd pay:** IT admins in restricted environments; security researchers; industrial/embedded hardware teams.

**Opportunity angle:** A lightweight, zero-config secure file bridge for restricted-network and air-gapped scenarios, with audit logging for compliance.

**Source:** [Show HN: PortButler – send files from macOS to a device with no way to receive](https://portbutler.sshlab.dev)

---

## ⚠️ Honest Assessment

The remaining posts (co-founder finding, AWS ECS vs EKS, Twitter alternatives, AI copyright, DNS adblocking) are either **too broad**, **too debated**, or **not monetizable pain points** — they read as discussion topics, not buying signals. They were dropped.

**Bottom line:** Only clusters 1–3 have meaningful evidence strength. Clusters 4–5 are speculative but have the right shape (specific ask + someone built something). Don't pad a roadmap around the rest.
