# Business Idea Clusters from HN/Reddit Data

---

## Honest Assessment First

This dataset is **thin**. Most posts have single-digit engagement and no excerpts to reveal actual pain points. Only **2–3 clusters** have meaningful signal. I'm not padding.

---

## Cluster 1: Cost-Efficient Always-On AI Agents

**Pain Point:** Running persistent/multi-model AI agents is prohibitively expensive at any real scale.

**Evidence Strength:**
- 3 related posts
- Combined engagement: ~16
- Asks are *specific* ("don't cost a fortune," "usable speeds," "multi-model agentic workflow setup")

**Posts:**
- [Ask HN: Is anyone running multi/always-on agents that don't cost a fortune?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50391647) — eng: 8
- [Ask HN: Can anyone run frontier AI models at usable speeds?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50394913) — eng: 2
- [Ask HN: How you guys setup multi-models agentic workflow](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50400117) — eng: 2

**Who'd Pay:** AI-native startups, solo devs building agents, small teams running automation pipelines. SaaS or infrastructure play.

**Potential Product:** A cost-aware AI agent orchestration layer — routes queries to cheapest capable model, sleeps idle agents, tracks spend per workflow. *(Note: FastVM's sleep-on-idle MicroVM concept is adjacent infrastructure.)*

---

## Cluster 2: Payment Infrastructure Trust & Reliability

**Pain Point:** Developers get burned by Stripe Connect edge cases and have no good way to vet payment infrastructure risks before committing.

**Evidence Strength:**
- 1 direct post, moderately specific
- Engagement: 19
- Warning posts on HN consistently get high engagement — this represents a recurring category

**Posts:**
- [Tell HN: Be Careful with Stripe Connect](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50398646) — eng: 19

**Who'd Pay:** Marketplace builders, platform companies using Connect, indie SaaS founders before they scale.

**Potential Product:** A "payment infrastructure risk & gotcha" guide/tool — think a structured database of edge cases, payout failure scenarios, and config audits for Stripe Connect/Braintree/etc. Could be a paid audit service or a SaaS config checker.

---

## Cluster 3: Self-Hosted / Open-Source Coding Agents for Ops

**Pain Point:** Teams want AI coding agents for incident response and backend tasks but don't trust or can't afford cloud-hosted solutions.

**Evidence Strength:**
- 2 related posts
- Combined engagement: ~8
- Both are concrete builds, not just discussions

**Posts:**
- [Show HN: Ryph – Self-Hosted, Open-Source Coding Agents for Incident Response](https://github.com/ryph-hq/ryph) — eng: 6
- [Show HN: Coding agent skills, tested on 15 backend tasks](https://github.com/globalmpc/backendskills) — eng: 2

**Who'd Pay:** DevOps/SRE teams at mid-size companies with data privacy requirements, regulated industries (finance, healthcare).

**Potential Product:** Managed self-hosted agent platform — open core, paid for the deployment/management layer. Similar to how GitLab attacked GitHub.
