# Business Idea Clusters from HN/Reddit Data

---

**Honest upfront note:** This dataset is thin. Most posts have very low engagement (2–7 points), no excerpts, and are one-offs. Only **3 clusters** have meaningful signal. I'm not padding to 8.

---

## 1. 🤖 AI Agent Trust & Security Infrastructure

**Pain point:** AI agents need credentials, take autonomous actions, and intercept network traffic — but there's no standard way to scope, audit, or approve what they do.

**Evidence:**
- "OpenAI Dots intercept SSL" (8 pts)
- MCP-Warden: credential broker so agents never see secrets (3 pts)
- MindThread MCP with per-permission approval (2 pts)
- "Does your company require human review of AI-generated code?" (4 pts)
- Rostr: give agents names, track who's working on what (3 pts)
- **5 posts, ~20 total engagement, highly specific asks**

**Who'd pay:** Enterprise security teams, dev-tool companies building on top of AI agents, compliance-heavy orgs (finance, health)

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50229493
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50241410
- https://github.com/ppcvote/mindthread-mcp
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50238496
- https://getrostr.sh/

## 2. 🧠 Agentic AI Bottlenecks / Tooling

**Pain point:** Developers building with AI agents keep hitting hard limits — context management, coordination between agents, and reliability — with no good tooling to address them.

**Evidence:**
- "What is the main bottleneck facing Agentic AI today?" (26 pts — highest non-enrollment post)
- "Are tab completion models dead?" (37 pts — signals frustration with current AI coding tools)
- Compactly: compact Claude Code sessions between tasks (2 pts)
- Mob programming with AI agents (2 pts)
- **4 posts, ~67 total engagement, specific technical frustration**

**Who'd pay:** Dev teams using Cursor/Claude/Copilot heavily, AI-native startups, engineering leads managing AI-augmented teams

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50234108
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50229834
- https://github.com/swapnildahiphale/compactly
- https://griyd.com

## 3. 🌱 OSS & Indie Product Distribution / Community

**Pain point:** Builders with good products (OSS or otherwise) have no reliable playbook for distribution — community building is the bottleneck, not the product itself.

**Evidence:**
- "Why a Great Product Won't Succeed Without a Community" (13 pts)
- "How do you grow OSS projects?" (5 pts)
- "How to promote my non-profit edtech platform?" (11 pts)
- 25launches: a Linktree for product launches (2 pts)
- **4 posts, ~31 engagement, recurring "how do I get users" anxiety**

**Who'd pay:** Indie hackers, OSS maintainers seeking sustainability, early-stage founders pre-PMF

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50227131
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50242424
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50234850
