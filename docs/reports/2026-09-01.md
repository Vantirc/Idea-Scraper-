# Business Idea Clusters from HN/Reddit Data

> **Honest note upfront:** This dataset is thin. Most posts have no excerpts, making signal extraction hard. Only **4 clusters** have genuine evidence worth acting on. The rest would be padding.

---

## 1. 🤖 AI Tool Reliability & Dependency Risk

**Pain point:** Teams are deeply dependent on AI tools but have no visibility into degradation, usage limits, or what breaks when AI goes away.

**Evidence strength:** 🟢 Strong
- 4 related posts
- ~143 combined engagement
- "Ask HN: What would happen if your company stopped using all AI tomorrow?" (100 engagement — highest in dataset)
- "Is it just me, or has Claude Opus gotten worse recently?" (14)
- "Claude 20x usage is only for the 5 hour window" (18) — users confused/frustrated by opaque limits
- "Are Prompt Injections Malware?" (11)

**Who'd pay:** CTOs, engineering leads at mid-size companies doing AI risk audits; compliance teams; anyone building on top of foundation models

**Opportunity angle:** AI dependency auditing tool, model performance monitoring/alerting, or a "AI continuity plan" SaaS

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49510066
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49519639
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49509882
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49506998

## 2. 🔐 Security Disclosure & Vendor Response Gap

**Pain point:** Developers who find security vulnerabilities have no good process or tooling to force vendor acknowledgment and response.

**Evidence strength:** 🟡 Moderate
- 2 related posts
- ~40 combined engagement
- "Dropbox Data Breach" (35) — ongoing concern about platform trust
- "What to do when a vendor doesn't respond to security issues?" (5) — very specific, actionable ask

**Who'd pay:** Security researchers, bug bounty hunters, corporate security teams, compliance officers

**Opportunity angle:** Structured vulnerability disclosure platform with automated vendor escalation, timestamping, and legal paper trails

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49507259
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49514427

## 3. 🛡️ AppSec False Positive Filtering

**Pain point:** Security scanning tools generate so many false positives that teams waste hours triaging noise instead of fixing real issues.

**Evidence strength:** 🟡 Moderate
- 1 direct post, but highly specific product signal
- 4 engagement (low, but "Show HN" launches often underengage)
- "Eatheria – Self-hosted AppSec platform with AI false-positive filtering" — someone already built this, suggesting real demand

**Who'd pay:** DevSecOps teams, security engineers at companies running SAST/DAST pipelines

**Opportunity angle:** AI layer on top of existing scanners (Snyk, SonarQube, etc.) that learns team-specific context to suppress known false positives

**Sources:**
- https://github.com/MartinCrespoC/Security-Team---Workspace-

---

## 4. 👨‍💻 SWE Career Transition Tooling

**Pain point:** Software engineers are anxious about AI displacing them and actively looking for structured "plan B" paths but have no dedicated resource.

**Evidence strength:** 🟡 Moderate
- 2 related posts
- ~20 combined engagement
- "What's your SWE career plan B?" (12) — high anxiety, clear community need
- "Is Vibe coding the frontend okay for someone expert in DevOps?" (8) — lateral skill transition question

**Who'd pay:** Individual developers ($10–30/mo), bootcamps or upskilling platforms as B2B, career coaches

**Opportunity angle:** Career transition navigator for engineers — skills gap analysis, adjacency mapping (DevOps → platform eng, etc.), income bridge scenarios

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49515138
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49518463
