# Validated Business Ideas from HN Data

---

## 1. 🤖 AI API Reliability & Fallback Infrastructure

**Pain point:** Developers paying for AI subscriptions (Claude, etc.) face outages, overload errors, and zero support — with no fallback.

**Evidence:** 3 posts, ~122 engagement
- "Paid Claude subscription unavailable >1 week, no support" (55)
- "Claude Code getting API Error 529 Overloaded" (7)
- Implied by heavy AI tooling discussion throughout thread

**Who'd pay:** Dev teams and businesses with AI in their critical path — they'd pay for a reliability layer, smart routing, or fallback orchestration across providers.

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49080775
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49067964

---

## 2. 🔊 Log Preprocessing / Noise Reduction for LLM Pipelines

**Pain point:** Sending raw, noisy logs to LLMs is expensive and produces worse results — there's no standard layer to clean and compress them first.

**Evidence:** 1 post, 41 engagement — but highly specific, working product shown

**Who'd pay:** DevOps/platform engineers at companies using LLMs for log analysis, incident response, or observability.

**Links:**
- https://github.com/ctrlb-hq/ctrlb-decompose

---

## 3. 🔐 Security Vetting for Open Source Projects

**Pain point:** Developers don't have a practical, fast way to assess security risk before running or installing unfamiliar GitHub projects.

**Evidence:** 1 post, 22 engagement — specific, recurring concern in the dev community with no great solution

**Who'd pay:** Individual developers and enterprise security teams doing open-source due diligence.

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49075417

---

## 4. ⏱️ Cron Job & Queue Worker Monitoring

**Pain point:** There's no obvious go-to tool developers trust for monitoring cron jobs and queue workers — people are still asking what others use.

**Evidence:** 1 post, 8 engagement — but an explicit "what do you use" ask signals an unmet or fragmented need

**Who'd pay:** Backend developers and small engineering teams running scheduled jobs in production.

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49067832

---

## 5. 🗺️ Warm Intro Follow-Up Tracking

**Pain point:** When you make a warm intro for someone, you have no visibility into what happened weeks later — the loop never closes.

**Evidence:** 1 post, 9 engagement — narrow but a real social/professional friction point

**Who'd pay:** Investors, connectors, recruiters, and sales professionals who make frequent introductions.

**Links:**
- https://www.getlooped.cc

---

## ⚠️ Honest Assessment

**Only clusters 1–2 have genuinely strong evidence from this dataset.** The rest are plausible niches with thin signals here — they're worth watching but shouldn't be acted on from this data alone.

**Dropped as noise:**
- BMW in-car ads (venting, no actionable angle)
- AI everywhere discourse (opinion, not a pain point)
- Country emigration thread (lifestyle, not B2B)
