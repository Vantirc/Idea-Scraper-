# Business Idea Clusters from HN/Reddit Data

---

## Honest Assessment First

This dataset is thin. Most posts have very low engagement (2–5), no excerpts, and lean toward venting or noise. Only **3 clusters** have meaningful signal. I'm not padding to 8.

---

## 🥇 Cluster 1: Security Vulnerability Tracking & Fatigue

**Idea Name:** Curated CVE/Vuln Intelligence Feed

**Pain Point:** Developers and security teams are overwhelmed by high-severity CVEs and RCEs, and lack a reliable, signal-to-noise-filtered place to track what actually matters.

**Evidence:**
- "Are we all tired of all these skybreaking CVEs/RCEs?" — 18 engagement
- "Where are you following security vulnerabilities?" — 5 engagement
- "GitLab 19.6.0 still vulnerable to Ruby2Shell and NGINX2Root" — 5 engagement
- **3 posts, 28 total engagement, specific asks**

**Who'd Pay:** Security engineers, DevOps teams, CTOs at SMBs who can't afford a full SOC. SaaS pricing $20–$99/mo per seat is proven in this space (Feedly for Security, Vulncheck exist but have gaps).

**Opportunity Angle:** Personalized, role-aware CVE digests filtered by your actual tech stack — not firehose alerts.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50552918
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50550926
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50561571

## 🥈 Cluster 2: Vibe Coding Workflow & Hosting for Non-Devs

**Idea Name:** Vibe Coding Deploy & Hosting Layer

**Pain Point:** Non-technical people are building apps with AI coding tools but have no clear, safe path to hosting and sharing what they've built.

**Evidence:**
- "How do you reach the flow with vibe coding?" — 16 engagement
- "Are your non-technical colleagues vibecoding? How are the apps hosted?" — 5 engagement
- "When does your app need its own UI vs. using it via MCP?" — 13 engagement (adjacent)
- **3 posts, 34 total engagement, recurring topic**

**Who'd Pay:** Non-technical "citizen developers" — ops managers, marketers, analysts — who build internal tools with Cursor/Replit/Lovable but get stuck at deployment. Also SMBs. $15–$49/mo is the right price band.

**Opportunity Angle:** A one-click "share this app" layer purpose-built for vibe-coded projects, with auth, usage limits, and no DevOps knowledge required. Think Vercel but the UX assumes zero technical background.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50548343
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50549762
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50554686

## 🥉 Cluster 3: Cheap, Transparent Cloud VM Pricing Comparison

**Idea Name:** CPU VM Price Intelligence Tool

**Pain Point:** Developers shopping for low-cost compute VMs have no easy, up-to-date comparison tool across the long tail of cloud providers.

**Evidence:**
- "Cheapest CPU VMs" — 21 engagement (highest in the dataset)
- **1 post, 21 engagement, very direct and specific ask**

**Who'd Pay:** Indie developers, bootstrapped startups, ML hobbyists running batch jobs. Monetize via affiliate/referral with cloud providers or a $5/mo "always-updated alerts" tier.

**Opportunity Angle:** A live, filterable comparison table (CPU, RAM, bandwidth, region, provider) that covers not just AWS/GCP/Azure but Hetzner, Vultr, OVH, Fly.io, etc. — updated automatically. Existing tools (cloudoptimizer, instances.vantage.sh) are partial or stale.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50554381
