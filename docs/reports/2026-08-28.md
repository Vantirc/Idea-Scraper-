# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Scan

---

## 1. 🤖 AI Code Quality & Agent Reliability

**Pain point:** AI agents produce code that gets thrown away — developers can't trust or verify output quality before it's too late.

**Evidence strength:** 3 posts, 33 engagement, highly specific asks
- "Last time an agent produced code you had to throw away, what had it got wrong?"
- "Show HN: Open tool for testing your AI Agents (No LLM)"
- "Show HN: Conduct, open-source guardrails for LLM and MCP tool calls"

**Who'd pay:** Engineering teams shipping AI-assisted code; devtool buyers at mid-size SaaS companies

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49477070
- https://github.com/IdoGol24/weir
- https://github.com/sseshachala/conductai

---

## 2. 🧠 Developer Identity & Skill Hedging in the AI Era

**Pain point:** Developers feel their skills and professional identity are being eroded by AI, and don't know what to learn or how to position themselves.

**Evidence strength:** 3 posts, 39 engagement, emotionally charged = high intent
- "Ask HN: AI writes better code than me. How to keep my identity?"
- "Ask HN: What new skills are you learning to hedge against AI"
- "Ask HN: Which class of business model is most resilient in the AI era?"

**Who'd pay:** Individual developers and tech leads anxious about career trajectory; bootcamps and career coaching platforms

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49481969
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49474923
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49472620

> **Opportunity angle:** Career coaching, structured learning paths, or a community product specifically for "post-AI developer identity" — not another coding course, but positioning/strategy content.

---

## 3. 🖥️ Lightweight Desktop App Framework (Electron Alternative)

**Pain point:** Developers hate Electron's bloat and resource usage but have no well-supported, production-ready alternative.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 16 engagement, very specific technical ask
- "Ask HN: Any true alternatives to Electron JavaScript?"

**Who'd pay:** Indie developers and small teams building desktop apps; companies paying cloud bills inflated by Electron memory use

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49478014

> **Note:** Single post but high specificity and 16 engagement on HN is meaningful. Known chronic pain point with prior market validation (Tauri exists but the question persisting = still unsolved for many).

---

## 4. 🔐 Local-First / Privacy-First Developer Tools

**Pain point:** Developers want capable tools (password managers, financial planners, AI clients) that don't phone home or require cloud accounts.

**Evidence strength:** 3 posts, 27 engagement, consistent theme
- "Show HN: Sesame - local-first, open-source password manager"
- "Show HN: A retirement planner in one HTML file, works offline, nothing uploaded"
- "Ask HN: Best open source way to run local open-weight LLMs in 2026?"

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-conscious developers, security teams, regulated industries (finance, healthcare)

**Links:**
- https://usesesame.app/
- https://www.torsalis.com
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49477063

> **Opportunity angle:** "Local-first" is becoming a real product category. A toolkit or platform for building local-first apps — or a suite of local-first productivity tools — has recurring demand.

---

## 5. 🛡️ Server Security Automation (Fail2ban Successor)

**Pain point:** Fail2ban is showing its age and developers want a modern, maintained alternative for intrusion prevention.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 8 engagement, very specific
- "Ask HN: Alternatives to Fail2ban?"

**Who'd pay:** Self-hosters, DevOps engineers, small SaaS teams without dedicated security staff

**Links:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49477766

> **Note:** Narrow but recurring DevOps pain point with clear willingness to switch products. Existing tools (CrowdSec) exist but the question persisting signals dissatisfaction.

---

## 6. 🚗 Vehicle Recall & Service Alert Monitoring

**Pain point:** Car owners don't proactively know about recalls or service bulletins affecting their vehicle until something breaks.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, 3 engagement — **weak signal**
- "Show HN: Dipstick Alerts – Search recalls and service bulletins for your car"

**Who'd pay:** Car owners; potentially auto insurance or fleet management companies as a white-label feature

**Links:**
- https://dipstickalerts.com

## ⚠️ Honest Assessment

**Only 3–4 clusters here have real evidence:**

| Cluster | Confidence |
|---|---|
| AI Agent Code Reliability | ✅ Strong — active tooling + clear complaints |
| Developer Identity / AI Career Anxiety | ✅ Strong — high emotion = high willingness to pay |
| Local-First Tools | ✅ Moderate — consistent theme, growing category |
| Electron Alternative | ⚠️ Moderate — chronic pain, single post here |
| Fail2ban Successor | ⚠️ Weak — one post, small audience |
| Vehicle Recall Alerts | ❌ Too thin to validate here |

The remaining posts (sticker auctions, Lisp compilers, Twitter viewer discontinuation) are **noise** and were dropped.
