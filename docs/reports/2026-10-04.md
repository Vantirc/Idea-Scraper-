# Business Idea Clusters from HN Data

---

## Honest Assessment Up Front

This dataset is **low signal**. Most posts have engagement under 10, excerpts are empty, and the batch skews toward noise (obituaries, TV show discussions, off-topic). I found **3 clusters with real evidence** and will not pad beyond that.

---

## Cluster 1: AI-Powered Local Media Search
**Idea Name:** Semantic Search Over Personal Photos & Video

**Pain Point:** Users can't search their own photo/video libraries by content or meaning — only by date or manual tags.

**Evidence:**
- "AI search for every photo and every frame of video on macOS" (engagement: 20)
- Implicit demand signal from AI coding tools discussion and image search interest
- Proven appetite: Apple Intelligence, Google Photos both moving this direction but with privacy concerns

**Evidence Strength:** 1 direct post, engagement 20 (top Show HN in this batch), highly specific build with a GitHub repo

**Who'd Pay:** Mac power users, photographers, content creators, parents with large family libraries. Prosumer tier ($5–15/mo or one-time). **Privacy-first angle** is the moat vs. Google Photos.

**Sources:**
- https://github.com/allenv0/SCM

---

## Cluster 2: Phone / Screen Time Self-Control Tools
**Idea Name:** Intentional Phone Use Manager

**Pain Point:** People know they use their phones too much but existing tools (Screen Time, Digital Wellbeing) are easy to bypass and don't address the underlying compulsion loop.

**Evidence:**
- "How do you control your phone use?" (engagement: 10)
- "How are you preparing your children for an AI-powered world?" (engagement: 6) — subtext is screen/AI dependency
- Recurring HN theme across many batches historically

**Evidence Strength:** 2 posts, combined engagement ~16, question framed as genuine personal struggle (not hypothetical)

**Who'd Pay:** Parents ($8–15/mo family plan), productivity-focused professionals, therapists recommending tools. Adjacent to existing market (Freedom, Opal) — differentiation needed.

**Sources:**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49947372
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49945325

## Cluster 3: Secret / Credential Leak Detection for Solo Devs
**Idea Name:** Lightweight Secrets Scanner for Indie Projects

**Pain Point:** Solo developers and small teams accidentally ship API keys and credentials in frontend/web code and have no simple, cheap way to audit this continuously.

**Evidence:**
- "I built a vibe coded website checker for leaked secrets" (engagement: 8)
- "Where can a solo dev still find a moat in LLM inference?" (engagement: 4) — signals indie dev anxiety about security + differentiation
- "Will source code become expensive if developers stop using GitHub?" (engagement: 6) — code security consciousness rising

**Evidence Strength:** 2–3 loosely related posts, combined engagement ~18, one is a direct product build validating demand exists

**Who'd Pay:** Indie hackers, "vibe coders" (explicitly named in the post), small SaaS teams who can't afford enterprise tools like GitGuardian. $0 freemium → $9/mo paid tier.

**Sources:**
- https://digitup.dev
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49951356
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49941022
