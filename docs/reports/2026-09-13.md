# Validated Business Ideas from HN/Reddit Data

*Honest assessment: This batch is thin. Most posts lack excerpts, making signal extraction hard. Only 3–4 clusters have genuine evidence.*

---

## 1. 🤖 AI Model Selection & Cost Control for Teams

**Pain point:** Engineering teams don't know which AI model to use day-to-day, and enterprise coding agent costs are spiraling out of control.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, combined engagement **166** — the highest in this batch. The model-selection question alone got 154 comments, indicating deep, unresolved debate. The enterprise cost-control question is very specific.

**Who'd pay:** CTOs, engineering leads at mid-size companies already using Cursor/Copilot/Claude Code. $20–100/seat/month for a policy/routing layer.

**Opportunity:** A "model router" or AI spend governance tool — routes requests to cheapest capable model, enforces budgets per team/project, logs costs by feature.

**Sources:**
- [What default model do you use and why?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49672966) (154 engagement)
- [Enterprise coding agents: controlling cost?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49673825) (12 engagement)

## 2. 🔏 Browser Privacy Transparency Tool

**Pain point:** Users don't know what data websites silently collect from their browser during a visit.

**Evidence strength:** 2 posts, combined engagement **39**. One is a working demo already. Reddit-blocking-Firefox story shows users actively fighting back against tracking — a motivated audience.

**Who'd pay:** Privacy-conscious consumers, enterprise IT/compliance teams, browser extension users. Freemium with a $5–10/mo privacy dashboard.

**Opportunity:** A consumer-friendly, always-on browser extension that surfaces real-time data leakage in plain English — fingerprinting, session data, inferred attributes.

**Sources:**
- [Everything a web page can learn about you](https://sessioncontext.org) (10 engagement)
- [Reddit blocking Firefox + uBlock Origin](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49673323) (29 engagement)

---

## 3. 📦 Offline / Private Voice Dictation

**Pain point:** Developers and power users want hold-to-talk voice dictation that works offline without sending audio to the cloud.

**Evidence strength:** 1 post, engagement **6**, but the fact someone already built and shipped this (cross-platform) signals real latent demand. Adjacent to a known frustrated market (Dragon NaturallySpeaking refugees, privacy-conscious devs).

**Who'd pay:** Developers, writers, healthcare workers bound by data privacy rules. $5–15/mo SaaS or one-time purchase.

**Opportunity:** Polished offline dictation app with hotkey activation, custom vocabulary, and local Whisper-based transcription. The open-source version here is rough — there's room for a clean commercial product.

**Sources:**
- [YazSes – offline hold-to-talk dictation](https://github.com/MSKazemi/yazses) (6 engagement)
