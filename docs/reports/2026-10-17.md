# Validated Business Ideas from HN Data

*Honest note: This dataset is thin. Only 2–3 clusters have meaningful evidence. I'm not padding the rest.*

## 🥇 #1 — Local LLM Hardware Decision Tool

**Pain point:** Developers want to know if their hardware can run a specific local LLM *before* spending money on it.

**Evidence**
- 1 post, engagement **134** (highest in dataset)
- Very specific, product-market fit demonstrated by the tool already existing and getting traction
- "Show HN" with real working product = validated demand

**Who pays:** Indie devs, AI hobbyists, small teams evaluating on-prem AI. Freemium with hardware affiliate links or a $5–10 one-time tool.

**Links**
- https://llmspeed.dev/

---

## 🥈 #2 — API Billing Safeguards / Spend Protection for AI APIs

**Pain point:** AI API providers (Anthropic, OpenAI) can silently overcharge by 100x due to bugs, with no hard spending caps or real-time alerts.

**Evidence**
- 1 post, engagement **123** (second highest)
- Extremely specific incident — internationalization bug caused catastrophic overbilling
- Classic "this shouldn't be possible" pain that recurs industry-wide

**Who pays:** Any developer or team using AI APIs at scale. A spend-monitoring middleware or budget-cap proxy layer. SaaS at $10–50/month per team. Also sellable to finance/ops teams at larger companies.

**Opportunity angle:** Build a lightweight proxy/wrapper that enforces hard spending caps, sends alerts, and logs anomalies across OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, etc.

**Links**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50121562

## 🥉 #3 — Better Documentation Tools (Anti-Confluence)

**Pain point:** Developers broadly hate Confluence — it's slow, hard to search, and not built for how engineers actually work.

**Evidence**
- 1 post, engagement **9** (low, but the *question* itself is a known recurring HN theme with historical high engagement)
- Weak standalone signal here, but corroborated by years of HN discussion
- Flag: treat as corroboration, not primary evidence

**Who pays:** Engineering teams at mid-size companies locked into Atlassian. Switches happen when a new CTO arrives. $10–20/user/month.

**Links**
- https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50123542
